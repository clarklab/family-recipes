Jalapeño Cornbread

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cornbread mix
  • 2 1/2 cups sweet milk
  • 1/2 cup salad oil
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 1 large onion, grated
  • 1 cup canned cream-style corn
  • 1 1/2 cups yellow cheese, grated
  • 1/2 cup jalapeño peppers (roasted, peeled and cooked)
  • Chopped crisp bacon
  • Chopped red pepper (canned pimiento may be used)

Directions

  1. Mix well, pour into 13" x 9" pan.
  2. Bake in preheated gas oven at 400° F. until done and brown, about 45 minutes.

Notes

  • Makes 24 two-inch squares.
