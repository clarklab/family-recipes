Chili

From the kitchen of Brother Goodman

Ingredients

  • 3/4 lb. hamburger
  • 1/2 of an onion
  • Salt & pepper
  • #2 can tomatoes
  • #2 1/2 can chili beans
  • 1 tsp. chili powder
  • Hot sauce or pepper, as desired

Directions

  1. Brown hamburger and onion in skillet. Add salt & pepper.
  2. Mix all together. Simmer slowly.
  3. Add hot sauce or pepper as desired.
  4. Cook very slowly.

Notes

  • From Brother Goodman.
