Ezekiel Bread

Ingredients

  • Wheat 2 1/2 c.
  • Rye 1 1/2 c.
  • Barley 1/2 c.
  • Millet 1/4 c.
  • Great Northern beans - 2 T.
  • Pinto beans - 2 T.
  • Kidney beans - 2 T.
  • Lentils - 1/4 c.
  • 2 t. salt
  • 1 1/4 c. warm water
  • 3 T. yeast
  • 1 t. honey
  • 1/2 c. corn oil
  • 1/2 c. honey
  • 1 c. warm water

Directions

  1. Mix the 1 1/4 c. warm water, yeast and 1 t. honey and let set until bubbly.
  2. After the above bubbles, add the corn oil and 1/2 c. honey.
  3. Now mix with dry ingredients, then add 1 c. warm water to all ingredients mixed together.
  4. Let rise in a pan 1 hr.
  5. Bake at 350° for 30 min. (Pat onto 2 cookie sheets with sides on them.)
