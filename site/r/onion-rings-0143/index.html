Onion Rings

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 1 c. buttermilk
  • 1 1/3 c. flour
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • 1/2 tsp. baking soda
  • Shortening, for cooking

Directions

  1. Beat egg & buttermilk together.
  2. Dip.
  3. Cook in hot shortening.

Notes

  • Another Onion Rings batter, made with milk and oil, is on card 0142.
