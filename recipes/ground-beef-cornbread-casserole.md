## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 pkg cornbread mix (no sugar)
- 1 - 17 oz can cream corn
- 1 egg
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 - 7 oz can chopped green chilis
- 8 oz. grated cheddar cheese

## Directions
1. Don't prepare cornbread by pkg. directions. Instead combine dry mix with can of corn & egg.
2. Pour 1/2 batter into buttered casserole dish.
3. Top with hamburger, add onions, chilis & cheese. Pour rest of batter on top.
4. Bake @ 350° for [40?]-50 min. till browned.
