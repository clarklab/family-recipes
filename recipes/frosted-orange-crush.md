## Ingredients
- 1 ripe banana, mashed
- 3 cups Orange Drink
- 1 cup unsweetened pineapple juice
- 1 pint Orange Sherbet

## Directions
1. Beat together banana, orange drink and pineapple juice until well blended.
2. Freeze until slushy.
3. Beat in orange sherbet. Pour into glasses.

## Notes
- Makes 4 servings (about 6 cups).
