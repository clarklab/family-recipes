## Ingredients
- 2/3 cup (small can) undiluted Carnation Evap. Milk
- 1 2/3 cups sugar
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1 1/2 cups (about 16 medium) diced marshmallows
- 1 1/2 cups (1 1/2 6-ounce packages) semi-sweet choc. pieces
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- Nuts, if desired

### Peppermint Fudge
- 1/4 cup coarsely broken peppermint stick candy

### Upside Down Coconut Fudge
- 1 cup flaked coconut

### Nut Fudge
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts, pecans or peanuts

## Directions
1. Combine Carnation, sugar and salt in saucepan over medium heat. Bring to boil, and cook 4 to 5 minutes, stirring constantly. (Start timing when mixture starts to "bubble" around edges of pan.)
2. Remove from heat. Add marshmallows, chocolate and vanilla. Stir vigorously for 1 min. (or until marshmallows are completely melted and blended).
3. Pour into 8-inch square buttered pan. Garnish with nuts, if desired. Cool. Cut in squares.

### Peppermint Fudge
1. Sprinkle 1/4 cup coarsely broken peppermint stick candy over top of fudge in pan.

### Upside Down Coconut Fudge
1. Spread 1 cup flaked coconut on bottom of buttered 8-inch square pan. Top with fudge.

### Nut Fudge
1. Add 1/2 cup chopped walnuts, pecans or peanuts to fudge mixture before pouring in pan.

## Notes
- From a Carnation evaporated milk recipe.
