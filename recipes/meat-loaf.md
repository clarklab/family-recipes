## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 lbs. ground beef
- 3/4 cup oats
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup chopped onions
- 2 tsp. salt
- 1/4 tsp. pepper
- 1 cup tomato juice

## Directions
1. Bake at 350° for 1 hour.
