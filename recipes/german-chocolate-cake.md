## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. Bakers German Sweet Choc.
- 1/2 cup boiling water
- 1 cup butter (shortening)
- 2 cups sugar
- 4 egg yolks, unbeaten
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 2 1/2 cups cake flour
- 1 tsp. soda
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 4 egg whites

### Coconut Pecan Frosting
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 1 cup sugar
- 3 egg yolks
- 1/4 pound oleo
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 1/3 cup coconut
- 1 cup chopped nuts

## Directions
1. Melt chocolate in 1/2 cup boiling water. Cool.
2. Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add egg yolks one at a time, beat well after each addition.
3. Add melted choc. and vanilla. Mix well.
4. Sift dry ingredients alternately with buttermilk to chocolate mixture. Beat until smooth.
5. Beat egg whites until stiff, peaks form. Fold into batter.
6. 3 layers. Bake in 350° oven, 35–40 minutes.

### Coconut Pecan Frosting
1. Combine evaporated milk, sugar, egg yolks, oleo and vanilla.
2. Cook and stir over medium heat until mixture thickens, about 12 minutes.
3. Add coconut and chopped nuts. Beat until cool and thick. Spread on tops.
