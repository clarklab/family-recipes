Peanut Butter Bars

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1 1/2 tsp. vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup firmly packed brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 1/4 cups flour
  • 2 tsp. baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp. salt

Directions

  1. Cream butter, peanut butter, and extract thoroughly; add the sugars gradually, creaming until fluffy. Add egg and beat thoroughly.
  2. Blend the remaining ingredients; add in thirds to creamed mixture, mixing until blended.
  3. Press into a 15 1/2 x 10 1/2 x 1 inch pan.
  4. Bake at 375° for 15 to 20 minutes.
  5. While warm, cut into bars or, if desired, into diamond shapes.

Notes

  • Makes about 4 1/2 dozen cookies.
