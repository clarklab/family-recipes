Banana Bread

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup shortening
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs, well beaten
  • 1 1/2 cups crushed bananas
  • 2 cups sifted flour
  • 1/2 tsp. soda
  • 1 tsp. baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp. salt
  • 1/4 cup pecans

Directions

  1. Cream together shortening, sugar and eggs.
  2. Combine in order given. Mix, beat with a few quick strokes.
  3. Put in greased and floured bread pan and bake 40 minutes at 350° F.
