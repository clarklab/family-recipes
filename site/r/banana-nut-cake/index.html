Banana Nut Cake

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup shortening
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 large mashed bananas
  • 1 cup broken pecans
  • 2 cups sifted flour
  • 1 tsp. soda (barely heaping)
  • 1/4 tsp. salt
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 tsp. vanilla

Directions

  1. Cream sugar, shortening and then eggs.
  2. Add dry mixture alternately with buttermilk and vanilla.
  3. Add bananas with mixer. Add pecans.
  4. Bake at 350° for 35 to 40 minutes in two 9" pans or at 325° for about 1 hour in angel food cake pan.
