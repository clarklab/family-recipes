Homemade Bread

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp. sugar
  • 2 Tbsp. shortening
  • 1 1/2 tsp. salt
  • 1 cup lukewarm water
  • 1 pkg. cake yeast
  • 3 1/4 cup flour

Directions

  1. Stir in 2 cups flour, then add till the right consistency.
  2. Bake 425 till brown.

Notes
