Popcorn Balls

Ingredients

No. 1

  • 3 quarts popped corn
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup white corn syrup
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/4 tsp. salt
  • 1 tsp. lemon or vanilla

No. 2

  • 3 quarts popped corn
  • 1 cup honey
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2/3 cup water
  • 2 Tbsp. butter
  • 1/4 tsp. salt

Directions

No. 1

  1. Place the popped corn into a large pan.
  2. Cook sugar, syrup and water to 280° F. Add flavoring and salt.
  3. Pour over the corn, stirring with a spoon so that all kernels will be evenly coated.
  4. Shape the corn into balls, lay on waxed paper to harden.

No. 2

  1. Proceed as for No. 1.
