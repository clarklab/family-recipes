## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup Bisquick
- 2 T. oleo
- 1 can (13 [oz.?]) evaporated milk
- 2 eggs
- 1 can (16 [oz.?]) pumpkin
- 2 1/2 t. pumpkin pie spice
- 2 t. vanilla

## Directions
1. Heat oven to 350°. Grease pie pan.
2. Beat all ingredients until smooth (1 min. in blender).
3. Pour into plate. Bake 50–55 min. until knife inserted comes out clean.
