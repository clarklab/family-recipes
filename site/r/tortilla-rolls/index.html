Tortilla Rolls

Ingredients

  • Large flour tortillas (6)
  • Soft cream cheese (large tub) or 1 1/2 stick, softened
  • Small can green chilies
  • Small jar pimentos
  • Garlic salt & Nature's Seasons to taste
  • Dash of paprika

Directions

  1. Mix together and spread on tortilla; roll.
  2. Wrap in wax paper. Refrig. overnight; cut.

Notes

  • Nature's Seasons is a Morton seasoning blend.
