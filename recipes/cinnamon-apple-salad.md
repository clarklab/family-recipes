## Ingredients
- 2/3 cup red cinnamon candies
- 2 cups boiling water
- 1 pkg. (6 oz.) strawberry flavored gelatin
- 2 cups cold water
- 2 cups (16 oz.) Meadow Gold Cottage Cheese
- 1 1/2 cups diced apples
- 1 1/2 cups chopped celery
- 3/4 cup chopped nuts

## Directions
1. Dissolve cinnamon candies in boiling water; pour over gelatin and stir until dissolved.
2. Add cold water; chill until partially thickened.
3. Fold in cottage cheese, apples, celery and nuts.
4. Spoon mixture into individual salad molds. Chill until firm.
5. Unmold on crisp salad greens. Serve with Meadow Gold Mayonnaise or Salad Dressing.

## Notes
- From a Meadow Gold dairy recipe.
- Makes 10–12 servings.
