## Ingredients
### 2 Pie Crust
- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 2/3 cups Crisco
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/3 cup ice water

### 6 Pie Crusts
- 4 1/2 cups flour
- 2 cups shortening
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup ice water

## Directions
1. Bake @ 425 to lt. brown.

## Notes
- "Lt. brown" means light brown.
