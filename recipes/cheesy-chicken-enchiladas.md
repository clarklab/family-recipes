## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 2 cups chopped cooked chicken
- 1 can (4 ounces) chopped green chilies
- 1/4 cup chicken broth
- 2 teaspoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 4 ounces Philadelphia Brand Cream Cheese, cubed
- 6 flour tortillas (6-inch)
- 1/4 pound Velveeta Process Cheese Spread, cubed
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 1/2 cup chopped tomato, divided

## Directions
1. Microwave onion, garlic and oil in 2-quart microwave-safe casserole on HIGH 2 to 3 minutes or until tender, stirring after 2 minutes.
2. Stir in chicken, chilies, broth and seasonings.
3. Microwave on HIGH 4 minutes or until thoroughly heated. Add cream cheese; stir until cream cheese is melted.
4. Spoon 1/3 cup chicken mixture onto each tortilla; roll up. Place, seam side down, in 8-inch square microwave-safe baking dish.
5. Microwave Velveeta Process Cheese Spread, milk and 1/4 cup tomato in medium microwave-safe bowl on HIGH 2 to 3 minutes or until Velveeta Process Cheese Spread is melted, stirring after each minute.
6. Pour sauce over tortillas; top with remaining tomatoes.
7. Microwave on HIGH 6 to 8 minutes or until thoroughly heated, turning dish after 3 minutes.
