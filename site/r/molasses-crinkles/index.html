Molasses Crinkles

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup shortening
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup molasses
  • 2 1/4 cup flour
  • 2 tsp. soda
  • 1/4 tsp. salt
  • 1/2 tsp. cloves
  • 1 tsp. cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • 1 to 1/2 cup raisins (if desired)
  • Sugar, for dipping

Directions

  1. Mix thoroughly shortening, sugar, egg and molasses.
  2. Sift together flour, soda, salt, cloves, cinnamon and ginger, and stir in.
  3. Add 1 to 1/2 cup raisins if desired.
  4. Roll into balls the size of walnuts. Dip tops in sugar; place sugared side up on greased baking sheet.
  5. Bake just until set but not hard. Baking 375 10 to 12 minutes.
