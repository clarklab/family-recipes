## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup cocoa
- 2 cups boiling water
- 4 tsp. soda
- 4 cups sugar
- 1 1/2 cups butter
- 6 eggs
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 5 cups flour
- 2 tsp. vanilla

### Icing for Cake
- 4 cups sugar
- 2 tsp. cream of tartar
- 4 Tbsp. cocoa
- 4 eggs
- 1 lb. butter
- 2 tsp. vanilla

## Directions
1. Mix cocoa, boiling water and soda, and let stand.
2. Bake at 400° for 20 minutes.

### Icing for Cake
1. Cook, stirring, until boiling, then until thick.
2. Pour immediately on cake. Spread with spoon.
