## Ingredients
- 2 lbs. hamburger
- Green pepper
- Onion
- Celery
- 1 qt. V-8 (Spicy Hot)
- 1 qt. chopped tomatoes (large can)
- 3 tablespoons honey
- 1/4 - 1/2 tsp. chili powder
- 1 can (veg. can size) red light kidney beans
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Directions
1. Brown hamburger, green pepper, onion and celery together.
2. In large kettle combine V-8, chopped tomatoes, honey, chili powder and kidney beans.
3. Add browned meat mixture to sauce. Salt and pepper to taste.
