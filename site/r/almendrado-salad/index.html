Almendrado Salad

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp. gelatin
  • 1/4 cup cold water
  • 1/4 cup boiling water
  • 1/4 tsp. cream of tartar
  • 1/4 tsp. salt
  • 6 egg whites
  • 2 tsp. vanilla
  • 1 cup sugar
  • Chopped almonds
  • Pink and green coloring
  • Custard sauce

Directions

  1. Soften 1 Tbsp. gelatin in 1/4 cup cold water. Dissolve in 1/4 cup boiling water. Cool.
  2. Add 1/4 tsp. cream of tartar and 1/4 tsp. salt to 6 egg whites; beat stiff.
  3. Mix in 2 tsp. vanilla and 1 cup sugar (2 Tbsp. at a time). Beat in gelatin.
  4. Divide in 3 parts. Add chopped almonds to one, tint one pink and one green.
  5. Pour layers into loaf pan lined with wax paper. Chill; slice.
  6. Serve with custard sauce.
