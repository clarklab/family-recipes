## Ingredients
- 3 tablespoons flour
- 2 3/4 cups sugar
- 1 can Carnation
- 6 eggs
- 1 Dream Whip
- 1 tbs Vanilla
- Pinch of salt

## Directions
1. Fill to top line of freezer container.

## Notes
- Makes 1 gallon.
- "Carnation" is Carnation evaporated milk.
