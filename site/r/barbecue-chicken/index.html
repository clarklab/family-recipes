Barbecue Chicken

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup margarine (1 stick, 1/4 lb.)
  • 1/2 cup sliced onion
  • 1 tsp. salt
  • 1/4 tsp. pepper
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 Tbsp. vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp. sugar
  • 1 Tbsp. Wor. sauce
  • 1/2 tsp. chili powder
  • 1/2 cup catsup
  • Chicken

Directions

  1. Pour over clean chicken. Bake 350° for about an hour.

Notes

  • "Wor. sauce" is Worcestershire sauce.
