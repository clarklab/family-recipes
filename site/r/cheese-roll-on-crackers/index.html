Cheese Roll on Crackers

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. Velveeta cheese
  • 1 1/2 pkg. cream cheese
  • 1 cup fine chopped nuts
  • 1/2 tsp. clove garlic or salt
  • 1 tsp. Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 tsp. red pepper

Directions

  1. Soften cheese; mix; roll in paper and chill.
