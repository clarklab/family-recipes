Fruit-Filled Chocolate Dreams

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup cold skim milk
  • 1 envelope (1.3 oz.) whipped topping mix
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon Hershey's Cocoa
  • Assorted fresh fruit, cut up
  • Chocolate Sauce (below)

Chocolate Sauce

  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup Hershey's Cocoa
  • 1 Tbls. cornstarch
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 1 Tbls. margarine
  • 1 tsp. vanilla

Directions

  1. Place foil on cookie sheet.
  2. In small mixer bowl, blend milk, topping mix, vanilla and cocoa. Whip at high speed of electric mixer until topping peaks, about 2 minutes. Continue beating 2 minutes longer until topping is light and fluffy.
  3. Spoon mixture into five mounds onto prepared cookie sheet. With spoon, shape into 4-inch shells. Freeze.
  4. To serve, fill center of each frozen shell with fruit; drizzle with Chocolate Sauce. Serve immediately.

Chocolate Sauce

  1. In small sauce pan, combine sugar, cocoa and cornstarch; stir in water.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until mixture comes to a boil; boil 1 minute.
  3. Remove from heat; add margarine and vanilla, stirring until smooth. Refrigerate.

Notes

  • From a Hershey's Cocoa label leaflet, "The Lean Way to Make it Chocolate! 8 Light & Luscious Recipes."
  • 5 servings. Chocolate Sauce makes about 1 cup. Per serving: 130 calories, 0 mg cholesterol, 1 g fat.
