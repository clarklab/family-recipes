Granola Bars

Ingredients

  • 3 1/2 cups oats
  • 1 cup raisins
  • 1 cup chopped nuts
  • 2/3 cup butter or oleo
  • 1/2 cup firmly packed brown sugar
  • 1/3 cup honey or molasses
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 tsp. vanilla
  • 1/2 tsp. salt

Directions

  1. Toast oats on a cookie sheet at 350° for 15–20 minutes.
  2. Combine oats with remaining ingredients. Mix well.
  3. Press firmly into a well-greased pan.
  4. Bake at 350° for 20 minutes.
