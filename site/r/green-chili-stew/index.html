Green Chili Stew

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs. pork (cubed) or meat
  • 2 – 8 oz. can stewed tomatoes
  • 2 cups cubed potatoes
  • 1 large chopped onion
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 3 cups chopped green chili
  • Salt to taste

Directions

  1. The card gives no method; it lists the ingredients only.

Notes

  • Written on "May His Love Touch Your Day" notepaper with a kitten and a ball of yarn.
  • "2 – 8 oz. can" means two 8-oz. cans.
  • If using leftover turkey, add turkey broth.
