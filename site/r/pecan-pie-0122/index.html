Pecan Pie

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup Karo syrup
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup pecan halves
  • 1 tsp. vanilla
  • 1 unbaked pie shell
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Beat eggs & sugar until thick.
  2. Add vanilla, Karo & salt. Mix; pour over nuts in shell.
  3. Bake at 300° for 50 to 60 min.

Notes

  • Written on the back of a menu flyer for Broasty Jr. restaurant in Clovis ("Next to Thunderbird Drive-In")
  • A different pecan pie recipe is on card 0121.
