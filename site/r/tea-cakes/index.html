Tea Cakes

Ingredients

  • 3 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 1/4 cups sugar
  • 5 tsp. baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tsp. vanilla
  • 1 cup shortening
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • Sugar, for sprinkling

Directions

  1. Sift all dry ingredients into large bowl.
  2. Cut in shortening, add unbeaten eggs and vanilla. Stir in thoroughly.
  3. Roll thin on floured board, cut and shape, sprinkle with sugar and bake in oven at 450°.

Notes

  • Amounts for a double batch are written in the margin: flour 7, sugar 2 1/2, baking powder 10, salt 1, eggs 6, vanilla 4, shortening 2, butter 1.
  • Amounts for a triple batch are also written in the margin: flour 10 1/2, sugar 3 3/4, baking powder 15, salt 1 1/2, eggs 9, vanilla 6, shortening 3, butter 1 1/2.
