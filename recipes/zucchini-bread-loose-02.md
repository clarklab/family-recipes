## Ingredients
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 2 1/2 cups flour
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 cup oil
- 4 eggs
- 1 tblspn. cinnamon
- 1 tsp. nutmeg
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1/2 tsp. ginger
- 2 cups zucchini

## Directions
1. Mix all dry and all wet, then mix together.
2. Bake at 350° for 1:15.

## Notes
- From [Robbie?] Peters.
- "1:15" means 1 hour 15 minutes.
