Twin-Cookie Sandwiches

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Domino Extra Fine Granulated Sugar
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • 3/4 cup soft butter or margarine
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp. grated lemon rind
  • 1 tsp. vanilla extract
  • 3 cups sifted all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp. double-acting baking powder

Filling

  • 1 egg white
  • 2 to 2 1/2 cups Domino Confectioners Pow. Sugar
  • 1 Tbsp. melted butter or margarine
  • 1/8 tsp. salt
  • 1/2 tsp. vanilla extract
  • Red food coloring

Directions

  1. Cream granulated sugar, salt and butter or margarine well. Blend egg, lemon rind and extract into mixture.
  2. Sift flour and baking powder together; stir into cream ingredients.
  3. Divide dough in thirds. Form each into a ball. Pat down on floured board and roll about 1/8" thick.
  4. Cut 2 3/4" hearts with floured cutter. Cut center from half of cookies with 1 1/2" heart cutter.
  5. Place all hearts (plain ones, cut-out tops and tiny hearts) on ungreased cookie sheets. Bake in moderate oven (350°F.) 7 - 8 minutes.

Filling

  1. Beat egg white and confectioners sugar. Add butter or margarine, salt and extract. Tint pink with food coloring.
  2. Make large sandwiches by spreading plain hearts and pressing cut-out tops on. Make tiny sandwiches by spreading filling between small hearts.

Notes

  • Makes 2 1/2 dozen large sandwiches and 2 1/2 dozen small sandwiches.
