Paper Bag Apple Pie

Ingredients

Filling

  • 1 unbaked nine-inch piecrust (homemade or ready-made)
  • 6-7 cups (about six apples) tart apples, peeled, cored and cut into thick slices
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice

Topping

  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup butter

Directions

Filling

  1. Place unbaked crust in pie pan. Prepare apples and put in bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, combine sugar, cinnamon and flour. Gently combine flour mixture with apples, and spoon into piecrust. Sprinkle with lemon juice.

Topping

  1. Combine brown sugar and flour, and then cut in the butter to create a coarse mixture (about the size of peas). Sprinkle mixture evenly over the top of the apples.
  2. Slide pie into a brown paper bag (like a grocery store bag), and fold the open end over twice and secure with large metal paper clips.
  3. Bake on a cookie sheet at 425° for one hour. Carefully split open the bag and allow pie to cool on wire rack.

Notes

  • Tip from the clipping: cooking inside the paper bag helps the apples heat thoroughly without overbaking the crust.
