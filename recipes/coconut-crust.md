## Ingredients
### Crust
- 2 cans (3 1/2 oz. each) flaked coconut
- 1/4 cup melted butter or margarine

### Filling
- 1/2 cup water plus pineapple juice
- 2 envelopes unflavored gelatin
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/8 tsp. salt
- 3 eggs, separated

## Directions
### Crust
1. Combine coconut and butter. Toss with a fork until the coconut is evenly moist.
2. Press against the sides of a 9" pie pan. Bake at 300° for 35–40 minutes.

### Filling
1. Measure liquid into a saucepan; sprinkle in gelatin. Let stand 1 minute.
2. Add half the sugar and all the salt, and [beat?].
3. Stir over low heat until gelatin is dissolved.
