Chocolate Pie

Ingredients

  • 2 tbs. cocoa
  • 2 tbs. cornstarch
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1 tbs. vanilla

Directions

  1. Save whites & beat for top of pie.

Notes

  • Added at the top of the card: "Crust 375 15 min" (bake the crust at 375° for 15 minutes).
