## Ingredients
- 4 - 5 lbs. ground beef
- 2 large onions
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 cups chopped celery
- 4 cans whole tomatoes
- 2 Tbsp. paprika
- 2 Tbsp. chili powder
- 4 cans tomato paste
- 2 cans mushroom sauce
- Salt and pepper
- 1/2 cup parsley
- 4 bay leaves
- 2 Tbsp. sugar
- 4 cups hot water

## Directions
1. Simmer 2 - 3 hours.

## Notes
- Makes enough for 4 meals.
