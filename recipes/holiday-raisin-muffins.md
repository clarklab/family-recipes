## Ingredients
- 2 cups sifted flour
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 Tbsp. baking powder
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1 cup chopped golden raisins
- 1 cup eggnog
- 1 egg, slightly beaten
- 1/4 cup butter, melted

## Directions
1. Sift together dry ingredients. Stir in raisins.
2. Combine eggnog, egg and butter; add to dry ingredients, mixing lightly.
3. Fill greased muffin pans 2/3 full.
4. Bake at 400° for 25 minutes.
