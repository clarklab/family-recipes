No Cook Magic Lemon Meringue Pie

Ingredients

Crust

  • 3/4 cup Kellogg's Corn Flake Crumbs
  • 2 Tbsp. sugar
  • 1/4 cup butter or margarine, melted

Filling

  • 1 can Borden's Eagle Brand Sweetened Condensed Milk
  • 1/2 cup lemon juice or bottled lemon juice
  • 1 tsp. grated lemon peel
  • 2 egg yolks

Meringue

  • 2 egg whites
  • 1/4 tsp. cream of tartar
  • 1/4 cup sugar

Directions

Crust

  1. In an 8-inch pie pan, blend together crumbs, sugar, and melted butter.
  2. Press firmly and evenly onto bottom and sides of pan. Chill.

Filling

  1. In a mixing bowl, blend milk, lemon juice, peel and yolks until thoroughly blended.
  2. Fill crumb crust.

Meringue

  1. In a small bowl whip whites with cream of tartar until they hold a soft peak.
  2. Gradually whip in sugar; continue to whip just until whites hold firm peaks.
  3. Pile onto pie filling and seal to inside edge of pie shell.
  4. Bake in 325° (slow) oven until golden brown, about 15 minutes. Cool.

Notes

  • Uses Kellogg's and Borden's Eagle Brand products.
  • Makes an 8-inch pie.
