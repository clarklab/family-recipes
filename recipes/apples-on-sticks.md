## Ingredients
- Small to medium apples (about 6)
- Skewers
- A few drops of cinnamon flavoring
- Bright red food coloring
- 1 recipe Glacé Syrup (below)

### Glacé Syrup
- 2 cups sugar
- 2/3 cup light corn syrup
- 1 cup water
- A few grains of salt

## Directions
1. Wash and dry apples; insert a skewer in the stem end of each.
2. Prepare glacé syrup. When syrup is removed from heat, flavor with a few drops of cinnamon and color bright red with food coloring. Stir as little as possible, but distribute flavoring and color evenly.
3. Holding an apple by the skewer, dip it quickly into the hot syrup, making certain the syrup covers it entirely. Remove and twist to spread the syrup evenly.
4. Place upright to dry in a rack which will keep the apples from touching. (A wire cake cooler is good.)

### Glacé Syrup
1. Combine ingredients in a small saucepan. Place over moderate heat and stir constantly until mixture begins to boil.
2. Cook without stirring to 300°F (brittle), using a low flame after 280°F in order to keep the syrup a light straw color.
3. As crystals collect on the sides of the pan, wipe them away with a damp cloth.

## Notes
- A Halloween treat.
- One recipe of glacé syrup covers six small to medium apples. Leftover syrup may be used for lollipops or candy squares.
