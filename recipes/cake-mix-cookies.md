## Ingredients
- 2 egg
- 1 box cake mix
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- Powdered sugar, for dusting

## Directions
1. Mix together cake mix, egg & oil in a med. bowl.
2. Make little balls with the dough and set on ungreased cookie sheets. Cover pan with parchment paper.
3. Bake at 375° for 8-10 mins.
4. Allow to cool, then dust lightly with powdered sugar.
