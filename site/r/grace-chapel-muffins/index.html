Grace Chapel Muffins

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour, unsifted
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 egg, lightly beaten
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 4 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1 cup fresh or frozen blueberries

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°. Sift together flour, baking powder, salt and sugar in a mixing bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, combine egg and milk, then add the melted butter.
  3. Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients and pour in milk mixture, stirring with fork just until dry ingredients are moistened. Fold in blueberries.
  4. Spoon batter into 12 buttered 3-inch muffin tins, filling each tin 2/3 full.
  5. Bake for 20 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Serve immediately or allow to cool.

Notes

  • A free recipe from the Grace Chapel Inn kitchen, on a Guideposts "Tales from Grace Chapel Inn" book flyer.
  • Makes 12 muffins.
  • Muffins may be frozen. To reheat, wrap in foil and place in a preheated 400° oven for 5 minutes.
