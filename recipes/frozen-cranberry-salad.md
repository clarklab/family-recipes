## Ingredients
- 1 qt. fresh cranberries
- 2 1/2 cups sugar
- 1 large can apricots, drain juice
- 1 large can peaches, drain juice
- 4 fresh apples
- 1 can crushed pineapple
- 3 cups pecans, chopped
- 1 pint whip cream

## Directions
1. Grind cranberries and sugar in food chopper; let stand overnight.
2. After grinding, add apricots, peaches and apples.
3. Then add crushed pineapple and pecans, and fold in whip cream. Then [freeze?].
