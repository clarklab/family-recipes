Frozen Salad

Ingredients

  • 1 Phil. cream cheese
  • 1 large can fruit salad, with its juice
  • Cut marshmallows
  • Nuts

Directions

  1. Combine cream cheese and the juice of the fruit salad.
  2. Add fruit salad, cut marshmallows, and nuts.
  3. Pour into ice cube tray and freeze.

Notes

  • "Phil." is Philadelphia cream cheese.
