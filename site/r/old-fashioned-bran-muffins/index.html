Old-Fashioned Bran Muffins

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all bran
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup soft shortening
  • 1 cup sifted flour
  • 2 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • 1/4 cup sugar

Directions

  1. 400° for 30 minutes.

Notes

  • "All bran" is All-Bran cereal.
