Hawaiian Kabobs

Ingredients

  • 1 can chunk pineapple, 20 oz.
  • 2 large green peppers, cut into 1 in. pieces
  • 1 large onion, quartered
  • 16–18 cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 Tbs. brown sugar
  • 2 tsp. ground ginger
  • 1 tsp. garlic powder
  • 1 tsp. dry mustard
  • 1/4 tsp. pepper

Directions

  1. Drain pineapple, reserving 1/2 cup juice.
  2. Place pineapple chunks & vegetables in a large bowl; set aside.
  3. In a saucepan combine pineapple juice with soy sauce, olive oil, brown sugar & seasonings. Bring to a boil; simmer uncovered for 5 minutes.
  4. Pour over vegetable mixture. Cover & refrigerate for at least 1 hr., stirring occasionally.
  5. Remove pineapple & vegetables from marinade. Grill kabobs for 20 min. or until soft.
