Hershey's Light Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/3 cup Hershey's Cocoa
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 6 tablespoons extra light corn oil spread
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup skim milk
  • 1 tablespoon white vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Light Cocoa Frosting (below)

Light Cocoa Frosting

  • 1 envelope (1.3 oz.) dry whipped topping mix
  • 1/2 cup cold skim milk
  • 1 tablespoon Hershey's Cocoa
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 350°. Spray two 8-inch round baking pans with cooking spray.
  2. Stir flour, cocoa and baking soda; set aside.
  3. In saucepan, melt corn oil spread; stir in sugar. Remove from heat. Add milk, vinegar and vanilla to mixture in saucepan; stir.
  4. Add dry ingredients; whisk until well blended. Pour evenly into pans.
  5. Bake 20 minutes or until wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean. Cool. Frost. Refrigerate.

Light Cocoa Frosting

  1. In small mixer bowl, stir together whipped topping mix, cold skim milk, cocoa and vanilla extract.
  2. Beat on high speed of mixer about 4 minutes or until soft peaks form.

Notes

  • From a Hershey's Cocoa label leaflet, "The Lean Way to Make it Chocolate! 8 Light & Luscious Recipes."
  • 12 servings. Per serving: 160 calories, 0 mg cholesterol, 4 g fat.
