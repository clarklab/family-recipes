## Ingredients
- Steak, cut small (1/4" or less)
- 85% ground chuck
- Water
- Spices
- Red tomato juice

## Directions
1. Cook the steak; pour the oil off and replace with water.
2. Add spices and cook [45?] minutes.
3. Add red tomato juice for color.
4. Add more spices as desired.

## Notes
- Written at the bottom of the card: "[ICS?] Trophy." ICS likely stands for the International Chili Society.
