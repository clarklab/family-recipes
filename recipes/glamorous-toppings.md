## Ingredients
### Coffee Topping
- Dream Whip
- 2 tsp. instant coffee
- 1 Tbsp. confectioners sugar

### Chocolate Fluff
- Dream Whip
- 2 tsp. sugar
- 2 Tbsp. instant chocolate mix

### Fruit Whip
- Dream Whip
- 1/4 cup diced or crushed fruit, such as strawberries or pineapple

### Crunchy Topping
- Dream Whip
- Crushed peanut brittle or peppermint candy

### Spiced Topping
- Dream Whip
- 1/2 tsp. nutmeg or cinnamon

## Directions
### Coffee Topping
1. Prepare Dream Whip, adding 2 tsp. instant coffee and 1 Tbsp. confectioners sugar to milk before whipping. Wonderful frozen, too.

### Chocolate Fluff
1. Prepare Dream Whip, adding 2 tsp. sugar and 2 Tbsp. instant chocolate mix to milk before whipping. Leftover topping is delicious frozen.

### Fruit Whip
1. Prepare Dream Whip and fold in 1/4 cup diced or crushed fruit, such as strawberries or pineapple.

### Crunchy Topping
1. Fold crushed peanut brittle or peppermint candy into prepared Dream Whip.

### Spiced Topping
1. Add 1/2 tsp. nutmeg or cinnamon to prepared Dream Whip. Wonderful on pumpkin pie.

## Notes
- Five ways to flavor Dream Whip whipped topping.
