## Ingredients
- 2 cups confectioners' sugar
- 1 cup vanilla wafer crumbs (about 30 cookies)
- 1 cup ground blanched almonds
- 1/2 cup Hershey's Cocoa
- 1/3 cup milk
- Cherry Tart Filling (below)
- Candied cherries, quartered, optional

### Cherry Tart Filling
- 1 package (3 ounces) cream cheese, softened
- 2 cups confectioners' sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon almond extract
- 1/2 cup coarsely chopped blanched almonds
- 1/4 cup candied cherries, quartered

## Directions
1. Paper-line 30 miniature muffin cups (1 3/4 inches in diameter); set aside.
2. In a large bowl combine confectioners' sugar, wafer crumbs, almonds and cocoa. Sprinkle in milk, mixing until ingredients are moistened and cling together.
3. Shape into walnut-size pieces; place in prepared cups. Press mixture against bottom and side of each cup to form shell. Chill.
4. Spoon Cherry Tart Filling into shells. Garnish with candied cherry quarters, if desired. Chill until firm.

### Cherry Tart Filling
1. Beat cream cheese in small bowl. Gradually add confectioners' sugar, vanilla and almond extract, beating until smooth.
2. Stir in almonds and cherries.

## Notes
- From an Ideals magazine flyer offering a free Hershey's Chocolate Cookbook.
- Makes about 30 tarts.
