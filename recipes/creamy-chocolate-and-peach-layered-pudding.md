## Ingredients
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup Hershey's Cocoa
- 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 2 2/3 cups 2% lowfat milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Peach Sauce (recipe follows)

### Peach Sauce
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 1 1/2 cups fresh peaches, pureed
- 1 tablespoon sugar

## Directions
1. In medium saucepan, stir together sugar, cocoa and cornstarch; gradually stir in milk.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until mixture comes to a boil; boil 1 minute.
3. Remove from heat; stir in vanilla. Press plastic wrap directly onto surface. Cool.
4. Meanwhile, prepare Peach Sauce.
5. In dessert dishes, layer chocolate pudding and Peach Sauce. Refrigerate until chilled.

### Peach Sauce
1. In small microwave-safe bowl, stir together water and cornstarch until smooth.
2. Add peaches and sugar; stir.
3. Microwave at HIGH (100%) 2 1/2 to 3 minutes or until mixture boils, stirring after each minute. Cool.

## Notes
- From a Hershey's Cocoa label leaflet, "The Lean Way to Make it Chocolate! 8 Light & Luscious Recipes."
- 6 servings. Peach Sauce makes about 1 1/3 cups. Per serving: 180 calories, 10 mg cholesterol, 5 g fat.
