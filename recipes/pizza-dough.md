## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. dry yeast (1 TB)
- 1 cup warm water
- 1 tsp. sugar
- 1 tsp. salt
- 2 TB salad oil
- 2 1/2 c. flour

## Directions
1. Dissolve yeast in warm water. Stir in remaining dough ingredients.
2. Beat vigorously about 20 strokes.
3. Allow dough to rest about 5-10 min.
4. Top with spaghetti sauce & mozzarella cheese.
5. Bake 450° - 12 min.
