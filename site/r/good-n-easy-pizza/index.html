Good 'n Easy Pizza

Ingredients

Dough

  • 1 package Red Star active dry yeast
  • 1 cup warm water (105 to 115°)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons salad oil
  • 2 1/2 cups Gold Medal flour*

Sauce

  • 1/2 cup chopped onion
  • 1 can (8 ounces) tomato sauce
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 teaspoon instant minced garlic
  • 1/8 teaspoon pepper

Topping

  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 teaspoons oregano
  • 1 cup sliced pepperoni (about 4 ounces)
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese (about 8 ounces)

Directions

Dough

  1. Dissolve yeast in warm water. Stir in remaining Dough ingredients; beat vigorously, about 20 strokes. Allow Dough to rest (about 5 minutes) while preparing Sauce.

Sauce

  1. Mix Sauce ingredients; set aside.

Topping

  1. Heat oven to 425°. Divide Dough in half. On lightly greased baking sheets, pat each half into 10-inch circle. (Continue to flour fingers when patting Dough into circles.) Spread Sauce on each circle.
  2. Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese and oregano. Arrange pepperoni on top and sprinkle with mozzarella cheese.
  3. Bake 20 to 25 minutes or until crust is brown and filling is hot and bubbly.

Notes

  • From a Gold Medal flour and Red Star yeast recipe card.
  • Makes 2 pizzas.
  • *If using self-rising flour, omit salt in the Dough. Unbleached flour can also be used in this recipe.
