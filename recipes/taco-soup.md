## Ingredients
- 1 lb. ground beef (1 1/2 lb.), browned
- 2 cans pinto beans, undrained
- 1 can diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 can corn, undrained
- 1 can Rotel or green chilies
- 1 pkg. dry Hidden Valley Ranch dressing
- 1 pkg. taco seasoning dry mix

## Directions
1. After meat is browned, add canned goods & dry pkg. Heat thoroughly.
