Sourdough Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup sourdough starter
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 cups lukewarm water
  • 2 level tablespoons sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tablespoons melted shortening
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp Baking soda

Directions

  1. 1/2 cup starter: add 2 cups flour, 2 cups lukewarm water. Beat until smooth & let stand in warm place overnight.
  2. To dough: add sugar, salt, melted shortening. Beat in eggs.
  3. Dissolve soda in tablespoon water, fold in gently. Do not stir after soda has been added.
  4. Grease griddle & fry.

Notes

  • Noted in the margin: "First Edition Arizona Cook book" (Booksmith, Tucson), likely where the recipe came from.
  • Written on the back of the Sourdough Biscuits card.
