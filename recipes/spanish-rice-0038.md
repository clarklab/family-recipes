## Ingredients
- 1 onion
- 1 whole green pepper
- 1 lb. hamburger, browned
- 1 can whole tomatoes
- 1 can tomato sauce
- 1 can each water
- Salt and pepper
- 3 bay leaves
- Garlic powder
- 1 Tbsp. curry powder
- 2 cups rice

## Directions
1. Simmer 1 1/2 hours.
2. Cook 2 cups rice and combine.
