## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup sourdough Starter
- 1 cup milk
- 2 1/2 cups unsifted flour
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 tsp baking pwd
- 1/2 tsp soda - scant
- Salad oil, butter, or grease

## Directions
1. Mix starter, milk & 1 cup flour. Prepare night before, cover & keep at room temp.
2. Turn this onto 1 cup flour on bread board.
3. Combine salt, sugar, baking pwd & soda with remaining 1/2 cup flour & sift over top.
4. Mix dry ingredients into dough, kneading lightly.
5. Roll out [1/2?] in thickness. Cut out, dip in warm grease.
6. Let rise for 1/2 hr. Bake in oven 375°. 14 biscuits.
