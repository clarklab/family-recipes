Canned Fruit Pie

Ingredients

  • 2/3 to 1 cup sugar
  • 4 tbs flour
  • 1/2 cup fruit juice

Directions

  1. Cook sugar, flour and fruit juice until thick.
  2. Pour over fruit. Bake 425°.
