Butterscotch Slices

Ingredients

  • 3 1/2 cups flour
  • 2 1/2 tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1/2 tsp. Salt
  • 1 1/2 tsp. Lemon Extract
  • 1 cup Chopped Nuts
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 1/2 c. Brown Sugar
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 1/2 tsp. Vanilla

Directions

  1. Add baking powder, salt and flour - sift.
  2. Cream butter, sugar. Add eggs, one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition.
  3. Shape in roll and roll in wax paper. Chill until firm.
  4. Bake 425°. 5 - 6 minutes.
