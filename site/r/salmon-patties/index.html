Salmon Patties

Ingredients

  • 1 can salmon (flaked)
  • 6 eggs (beaten)
  • 1/4 box saltine crackers (crushed)
  • Lemon juice
  • Garlic salt
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Chopped onions

Directions

  1. Mix salmon, eggs and crackers in pan.
  2. Add lemon juice, garlic salt, salt, black pepper and chopped onions to flavor taste.
  3. Fry into hot veg. oil until brown both sides.
