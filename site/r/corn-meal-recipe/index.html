Corn Meal Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup corn meal
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • 2 tsp. baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg beaten
  • 3 Tbsp. cooking oil
  • 1 Tbsp. sugar

Directions

  1. Mix dry ingredients in a bowl. Add milk and egg, stir together.
  2. Add cooking oil or melted shortening and thoroughly mix.
  3. Pour into an 8 inch round cake pan and bake at 450° F. for 20 to 25 minutes.
  4. For corn sticks use same recipe and be sure pan is hot before pouring in batter.
