Egg Sauce for Lettuce or Cabbage

Ingredients

  • 4 Tb. sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 Tb. vinegar
  • Sweet cream

Directions

  1. Cook til thick.
  2. Add sweet cream; mix and serve on lettuce.
