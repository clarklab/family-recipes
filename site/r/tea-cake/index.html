Tea Cake

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Crisco
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup milk
  • 4 eggs
  • 3 cups flour
  • 2 tsp. vanilla
  • 1 tsp. baking powder

Directions

  1. Roll and bake at 350°.

Notes

  • Amounts for a quadruple batch are written in the margin: 4 Crisco, 8 sugar, 4 milk, 16 eggs, 12 flour, 8 vanilla, 4 baking powder.
