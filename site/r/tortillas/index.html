Tortillas

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tsp. baking powder
  • 3/4 tsp. salt
  • 3 tablespoons shortening - oil
  • 1 1/2 cups cold water

Directions

  1. In a bowl combine ingredients.
  2. Cover w/ a damp towel for 30 min.
  3. Knead 5 min. on lightly floured surface.
  4. Shape dough into 5 balls of equal size and roll out to desired round size.
  5. Put on heated griddle to cook.
