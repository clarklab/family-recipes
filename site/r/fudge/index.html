Fudge

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sugar
  • 2 Tbsp cocoa per cup sugar (4 Tbsp)
  • 1 cup milk
  • Dash salt
  • 1 1/2 tsp vanilla

Directions

  1. Stir and cook until it gets thick, soft ball.
  2. Turn out fire, add vanilla.
  3. Beat until right consistency, pour in buttered dish.
  4. Cut before completely hardened.

Notes

  • Added in green ink in the margin: 2 Tbsp butter, 1/4 tsp cream of tartar, 2 Tbsp Karo.
  • Also added in green ink: "Cool to lukewarm" before beating.
