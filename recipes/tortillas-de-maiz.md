## Ingredients
- 2 cups blue or yellow corn masa harina
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 2/3 cups boiling water

## Directions
1. Combine corn masa harina and salt in mixing bowl.
2. Add boiling water and stir until dough resembles thick cooked cereal. Set dough aside for 30 minutes.
3. Wet hands lightly and form dough into balls the size of hen's eggs.
4. Place each ball of dough between two very lightly moistened pieces of waxed paper and flatten to about 1/8 inch using a tortilla press, rolling pin, or pressure from hand.
5. Remove tortilla from waxed paper and bake on lightly greased pre-heated electric skillet, turning to very lightly brown tortilla on both sides.

## Notes
- From a Clovis, New Mexico newspaper clipping, made in an electric skillet.
- Makes 12–14 tortillas. Heat the skillet to 420°F.
- Tip from the clipping: one-half cup all-purpose flour may be added to make the dough easier to handle (add slightly more water).
