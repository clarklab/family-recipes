## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup shortening (melted)
- 1/2 cup boiling water
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 3 Tbsp. sugar
- 1/2 cup cold water
- 1 pkg. yeast
- 1 egg
- 3 cups flour

## Directions
1. Mix the shortening, boiling water, salt and sugar, and add the cold water, yeast, egg and flour.
2. Let rise and punch down; let rise and roll out; let rise double and fry.
3. Dip in powdered sugar mixture.
