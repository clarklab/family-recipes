Barbecued Round Steak

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds round steak
  • 1/4 cup seasoned flour
  • 3 Tbsp. shortening
  • 1/3 cup minced onion
  • 1/3 cup minced celery
  • 1 small clove garlic
  • 1 can (10 1/2 oz.) tomato soup
  • 2 Tbsp. brown sugar
  • 2 Tbsp. Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 Tbsp. lemon juice
  • 2 tsp. prepared mustard
  • 1/4 tsp. Tabasco sauce

Directions

  1. Pound flour into steak; cut into 3" squares.
  2. Melt shortening in skillet on gas burner with a brain set at 250°; brown steak with onion, celery and garlic.
  3. Combine remaining ingredients; pour over steak.
  4. Cover and cook on gas burner with a brain set at 225° or in a gas oven at 350° for about 1 1/2 hours or until tender.

Notes

  • Serves 6.
  • A "gas burner with a brain" is a thermostat-controlled gas stove burner, an old gas-company phrase.
