## Ingredients
- 1 1/4 cup flour
- 2 tbs. sugar
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1 egg (beaten)
- 1 tbs. Wesson oil
- 1 cup milk

## Directions
1. Leave slightly lumpy.
2. Cook with bacon grease.
