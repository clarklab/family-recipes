## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup Blue Bonnet Margarine
- 1 clove garlic, mashed
- 1/4 cup grated onion
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce
- 1 Tbsp. dry mustard
- 1 cup chili sauce
- 1/4 tsp. Tabasco

## Directions
1. Melt Blue Bonnet Margarine in saucepan. Add garlic and onion and stir over medium heat for 2 minutes.
2. Add remaining ingredients, stirring until sauce bubbles and thickens slightly.
3. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer gently about 20 minutes.

## Notes
- From a Blue Bonnet Margarine recipe.
- Makes 1 pint.
