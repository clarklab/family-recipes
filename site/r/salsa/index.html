Salsa

Ingredients

  • 1 can tomatoes
  • 1/2 onion, cut up
  • 3 pods of garlic, cut up
  • Jalapeños (optional) - 1 to start

Directions

  1. Mix ingredients in blender. Blend to liquid - completely (no pieces).
