## Ingredients
- 2 bacon strips, diced
- 1 pound ground beef or venison
- 2 teaspoons chili powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic salt
- 1/4 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/8 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 3 to 5 drops hot pepper sauce
- 1 can (14 1/2 ounces) diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 cup each finely chopped celery, onion and carrots
- 1/2 cup finely chopped green pepper
- 1 can (16 ounces) chili beans, undrained

## Directions
1. In a large saucepan over medium heat, brown bacon and the beef or venison; drain.
2. Add the seasonings; cook and stir for 5 minutes.
3. Stir in tomatoes, celery, onion, carrots and green pepper; bring to a boil.
4. Reduce heat; cover and simmer for 40 minutes.
5. Stir in beans; cook 30 minutes longer.

## Notes
- From a magazine clipping.
- Makes 6 servings.
