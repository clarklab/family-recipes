## Ingredients
- Roast
- 2 c. pinto beans
- 1 can green chilies
- 1 can tomatoes
- 1 can tomato sauce
- 1 onion, chopped

## Directions
1. Cover with water. Season to taste.
2. Cook at 250° for 12 hrs.
