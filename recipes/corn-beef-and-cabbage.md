## Ingredients
- Cabbage
- Onion
- Canned corn beef
- Chopped celery
- Carrots (4-5), quartered
- Can tomato sauce
- 1 can water
- Salt and pepper

## Directions
1. Cut cabbage in 1/4's.
2. Simmer onion and canned corn beef.
3. Add chopped celery and quartered carrots (4-5).
4. Add can tomato sauce and 1 can water.
5. Add cabbage, salt and pepper. Cover and let boil. Simmer until tender.
