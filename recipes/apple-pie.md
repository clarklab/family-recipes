## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 Tbsp. flour
- 1/2 tsp. cinnamon
- Dash of salt
- 1 (No. 2) can sliced pie apples or 6 med. size tart fresh
- 1/4 cup butter
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup unsifted flour

## Directions
1. Blend together first 4 ingredients. Toss lightly with apples.
2. Spoon into pastry-lined 9" Bake-A-Pie.
3. Combine butter, brown sugar and 1/2 cup flour. Sprinkle over pie filling.
4. Cover with top crust. Bake in 425° oven 35–40 minutes or until brown.

## Notes
- Tip on the card: For best results bake pies in center of oven. Do not use Bake-A-Pie plates for baking pie shells. (Bake-A-Pie was a brand of pie plate.)
