Chicken Pie

Ingredients

  • 4–5 lb. stewing hen
  • Boiling water
  • 2 parsley sprigs
  • 3–4 stalks celery
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 sliced onion
  • 1 Tbsp. salt
  • Dash of pepper
  • 2 cups pearl onions
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1/2 cup flour for each 4 cups broth
  • 1 cup milk
  • Salt and pepper to season
  • Pie crust or biscuit dough

Directions

  1. Cut up stewing hen, place in kettle. Cover with boiling water.
  2. Add to kettle parsley sprigs, celery, carrot, sliced onion, 1 Tbsp. salt, and a dash of pepper.
  3. Boil 2 minutes, lower heat and simmer gently 2–3 hours.
  4. Cool, remove meat from bones and place in casserole. Add pearl onions and peas.
  5. Thicken broth by straining broth from chicken and to each 4 cups broth add 1/2 cup flour, combined with 1 cup milk to form paste. Cook until thickened. Season with salt, pepper.
  6. Cover casserole with pie crust or biscuit dough. Make large slits in crust.
  7. Bake at 450° until crust browns and serve at once.

Notes

  • Serves 6–8.
