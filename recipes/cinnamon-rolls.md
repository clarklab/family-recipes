## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 c. flour
- 1/3 c. sugar
- 1 tsp. salt
- 2 TBp yeast
- 1 c. scalded milk
- 1/2 c. water
- 1/4 c. melted butter
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1 egg
- 2 cups flour

### Cinnamon Mixture
- 1 stick of butter, softened
- 1 c. brown sugar
- Optional: raisins, nuts

## Directions
1. Place 1 1/2 c. flour, sugar, salt and yeast in a mixing bowl & stir.
2. Pour scalded milk, water and melted butter to dry ingredients and add 1/2 cup flour and 1 egg. Mix.
3. Gradually add 2 cups of flour; knead/stir.
4. Let rise 15-20 minutes.
5. Roll out on lightly floured surface.
6. Spread with cinnamon mixture.
7. Roll up dough and cut in circles.
8. Place in greased pan.
9. Let rise till doubled (20 min).
10. Bake 15-20 min. at 400°; cooking time varies.
