Finest Cherry Pie

Ingredients

  • Pastry for a 2-crust 9-inch pie
  • 1 Tbsp. sugar
  • 1 Tbsp. cake flour
  • 1 can (No. 303) Stokely's Finest Red Tart Pitted Cherries
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup cake flour
  • 1/8 tsp. salt
  • 1/4 tsp. almond extract
  • 1 tsp. lemon juice
  • 1 Tbsp. butter

Directions

  1. Line a 9-inch pie pan with pastry. Sprinkle with 1 Tbsp. sugar and 1 Tbsp. cake flour.
  2. Drain cherries, saving the juice.
  3. Combine sugar, flour and salt. Gradually stir in cherry juice. Add almond extract and lemon juice.
  4. Combine with cherries and pour into pastry-lined pan. Dot with butter. Put top crust in place.
  5. Bake 30 to 35 minutes at 425°.

Notes

  • This recipe is for the 1-lb. size can.
