Watermelon Rind Preserves

Ingredients

  • 4 lbs. prepared watermelon rinds, cut in inch cubes
  • Lime water to cover

Syrup

  • 7 cups sugar
  • 2–3 cups syrup (white)
  • 6 cups water

Directions

  1. Cut 4 lbs. prepared watermelon rinds in inch cubes. Lime water to cover.
