## Ingredients
- 1 cup butter or mar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 beaten eggs
- 1/2 teaspoon soda
- 3 tbs thick sour cream
- 3 cups sifted flour
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

## Directions
1. Cream butter w. sugar.
2. Dissolve soda in sour cream.
3. Bake @ 400° 5 min or until golden brown.

## Notes
- "Mar" is short for margarine.
