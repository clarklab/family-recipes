Beef Pie

Ingredients

  • 1 med. onion
  • 2 lb. butter
  • 1 can mushroom soup
  • 1 tsp. steak sauce
  • 1 cup diced potatoes
  • 1/2 cup beans
  • 1 cup meat (beef), cut up
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • Biscuits

Directions

  1. Cook butter and onions in skillet.
  2. Cut up 1 cup meat; mix beans, 1/2 cup milk, soup.
  3. Add diced potatoes, 1/2 cup green beans, 1 cup beef. Simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Place biscuits on top. Bake.
