Orange Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/4 tsp. salt
  • 2 tsp. cornstarch
  • 1 cup orange juice
  • 1 cup boiling water
  • 1 Tbsp. butter
  • 2 tsp. grated orange rind

Directions

  1. Mix in saucepan sugar, salt, and cornstarch.
  2. Stir in orange juice and boiling water.
  3. Boil 1 min., stirring constantly.
  4. Remove from heat, add butter and grated orange rind.

Notes

  • The card describes it as a "clear orange sauce."
