Pineapple Upside-Down Cake

Ingredients

Topping

  • 3 Tbsp. butter or margarine
  • 1 cup C and H Light Brown Sugar*
  • Pineapple slices (or chunks, tidbits, or crushed, slightly drained)
  • Nuts
  • Maraschino cherries

Cake Batter

  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 1 cup C and H Granulated Sugar*
  • 1/4 tsp. salt
  • 1/2 cup hot pineapple syrup
  • 1 cup cake flour
  • 1 tsp. baking powder

To Serve

  • Whipped cream

Directions

Topping

  1. Melt 3 Tbsp. butter or margarine in a 9" square cake pan. Sprinkle 1 cup C and H Light Brown Sugar over bottom.
  2. Arrange pineapple slices (or chunks, tidbits, or crushed, slightly drained) with nuts and Maraschino cherries over sugar to make a pretty design.
  3. Put into oven to warm while you mix the cake batter.

Cake Batter

  1. Beat egg yolks until light. Gradually beat in sugar, salt and hot pineapple syrup.
  2. Stir in flour sifted with baking powder. Fold in stiffly beaten egg whites.
  3. Pour over fruit mixture. Bake at 350° (moderate) 35 to 45 minutes, or until done.
  4. Turn out. Top with whipped cream.

Notes

  • A C and H Sugar recipe. The sugars are starred, with the footnote "*.....pure CANE, of course."
  • Makes 6 to 9 servings.
