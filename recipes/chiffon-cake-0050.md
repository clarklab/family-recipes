## Ingredients
- 2 cups flour
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 3 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1/2 cup cooking oil
- 7 unbeaten egg yolks
- 3/4 cup cold water
- 7 or 8 egg whites
- 1/2 tsp. cream of tartar
- Flavor

## Directions
1. Make well and add oil, unbeaten egg yolks and cold water. Beat until smooth.
2. Whip 7 or 8 egg whites with 1/2 tsp. cream of tartar until very stiff. Add flavor.
3. Fold in flour.
4. Bake at 325° for 1 hr.
