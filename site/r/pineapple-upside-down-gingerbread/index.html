Pineapple Upside-Down Gingerbread

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar (packed)
  • 1/3 cup candied cherry halves or pecan halves
  • 6 drained Del Monte Pineapple Slices
  • 1 pkg. Betty Crocker Gingerbread Mix

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 350°.
  2. Melt butter in 9" round or 9" sq. pan. Blend in sugar. Spread in pan; sprinkle with cherry or pecan halves. Arrange pineapple slices over bottom of pan.
  3. Make batter as directed on Betty Crocker package. Pour into prepared pan.
  4. Bake 40 to 45 minutes. Let cake stand a few minutes before inverting on plate. Serve warm.
