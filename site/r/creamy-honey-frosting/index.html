Creamy Honey Frosting

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp. shortening
  • 1 1/2 cups confectioners' sugar (sift if lumpy)
  • 1/4 tsp. salt
  • 1/2 tsp. vanilla
  • 2 Tbsp. honey
  • 1 Tbsp. milk

Directions

  1. Combine shortening, salt, vanilla, and 1/2 cup sugar.
  2. Add remaining sugar and honey and milk alternately, mixing until smooth and creamy.
  3. Add more sugar to thicken or milk to thin frosting, if required, until of spreading consistency. Spread on cake.
