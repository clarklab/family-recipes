## Ingredients
- 4 1/2 cups of juice
- 1/2 cup lemon juice
- 1 box Sure-Jell
- [6?] 1/2 cups of sugar

## Directions
1. Combine juice, Sure-Jell, lemon juice. Bring to a fast boil.
2. Add sugar. When it comes to a fast boil that cannot be stirred down, boil 2 minutes.
3. Remove from fire, skim, pour up. Seal with lids or paraffin.
