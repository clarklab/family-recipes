Corn Pone for Dressing

Ingredients

Corn Pone

  • 3 cups corn meal
  • 3 cups flour
  • 2 tsp. salt
  • 8 tsp. baking powder
  • 4 1/2 cups milk
  • 1/4 cup cooking oil
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 tbs. sage

Dressing

  • Broth from turkey
  • Onion
  • Celery
  • Eggs

Directions

Corn Pone

  1. Bake @ 425° 1 hour.

Dressing

  1. Add to broth from turkey.
  2. Sauté onion, celery. Add eggs.

Notes

  • Another version is on card 0097, which adds a gravy, amounts for a larger batch, and fuller dressing steps.
