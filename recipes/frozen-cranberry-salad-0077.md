## Ingredients
- 1 qt fresh cranberries
- 2 1/2 cups sugar
- 1 apricots (large can), drain juice
- 1 peaches (large can), drain juice
- 4 cup fresh apples
- 1 can crushed pineapple
- 3 cups pecans
- 1 pt whipped cream

## Directions
1. Grind cranberries and sugar in food chopper & let stand overnight.
2. Add after grinding: apricots, peaches, apples, crushed pineapple and pecans.
3. Mix all ingredients together & fold in whipped cream.
4. Freeze.
