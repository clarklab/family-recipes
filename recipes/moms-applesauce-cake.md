## Ingredients
- 2 cups sugar
- 3 cups stewed apples
- 4 cups flour
- 1 cup raisins
- 1 T. cocoa powder
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 1 cup nuts (broken)
- 1 cup butter
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 1 tsp. allspice
- 1 tsp. cloves
- Sprinkle of nutmeg
- 4 tsp. soda

## Directions
1. Cream apples, butter & sugar in large bowl. Add spices & salt.
2. Stir in raisins & nuts. Cocoa.
3. Add flour 1 cup at a time. Add soda last.
4. Bake in large Bundt pan until toothpick comes out clean, about an hour in 350° oven.
