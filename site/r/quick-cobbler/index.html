Quick Cobbler

Ingredients

  • 1 stick of butter
  • 3 cups sweetened fruit (apples, blueberries, whatever)
  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 ts. baking powder
  • 1 ts. salt
  • 1 cup milk

Directions

  1. Melt stick of butter in cobbler pan.
  2. Add 3 cups sweetened fruit.
  3. Mix flour, baking powder, salt & milk. Pour over fruit.
  4. Cook at 400° till golden. Delicious!

Notes

  • Shared at the end of a family letter to "Mommie & Daddy," which calls it "a delicious QUICK recipe for a cobbler."
