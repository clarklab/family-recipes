## Ingredients
### I
- 2 cups sifted flour (spoon lightly into cup)
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 3 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1/2 cup cooking oil
- 7 unbeaten egg yolks
- 3/4 cup cold water

### II
- Egg whites (7 or 8 – 1 cup)
- 1/2 tsp cream of tartar

## Directions
### I
1. Sift together flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. Make a well and add oil, egg yolks and cold water.
2. Beat with spoon until smooth.

### II
1. Beat egg whites and cream of tartar until very stiff.
2. Pour egg yolk mixture gradually over whites; fold in.
3. Bake 325° 55 minutes.
