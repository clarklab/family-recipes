Magic Topping

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup coconut
  • 3/4 cup light brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • 1/3 cup shortening
  • 2 Tbsp. milk

Directions

  1. Combine ingredients and cook, stirring constantly, until sugar melts.
  2. Spread on top of warm cake. Return to 375° oven for 10–12 min. until lightly browned.
