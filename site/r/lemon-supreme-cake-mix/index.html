Lemon Supreme Cake Mix

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • Lemon Jello pudding
  • 1/4 cup Crisco oil
  • 2/3 cup water
  • 1 tsp. lemon extract

Topping

  • 1 lemon juice
  • 2 tab butter
  • 1 1/2 cups powdered sugar

Directions

  1. Mix together.

Topping

  1. Melt, add lemon juice, sugar. Punch holes. Spoon in glaze.

Notes

  • "1 lemon juice" means the juice of 1 lemon.
