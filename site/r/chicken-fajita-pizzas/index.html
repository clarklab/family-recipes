Chicken Fajita Pizzas

Ingredients

  • 4 flour tortillas (6-inch)
  • 1 1/2 cups (6 ounces) Velveeta Mexican Shredded Process Cheese Food
  • 1 cup shredded cooked chicken
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 1/2 cup chopped green bell pepper
  • 1/2 cup seeded chopped tomato
  • 1/4 cup pitted ripe olive slices

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 350°.
  2. Place tortillas on cookie sheet; sprinkle 2 tablespoons Velveeta Mexican Shredded Process Cheese Food on each tortilla.
  3. Bake 4 to 5 minutes or until Velveeta Mexican Shredded Process Cheese Food melts.
  4. Toss chicken with salsa; spread on tortillas.
  5. Toss green bell peppers with remaining Velveeta Mexican Shredded Process Cheese Food; sprinkle over chicken mixture.
  6. Bake 7 to 10 minutes or until thoroughly heated and Velveeta Mexican Shredded Process Cheese Food is melted.
  7. Top with remaining ingredients.

Notes

  • From a Kraft Velveeta recipe leaflet. Makes 4 servings. Prep time: 10 minutes. Cooking time: 15 minutes.
