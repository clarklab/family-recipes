## Ingredients
- 2 pounds beef (coarse grind)
- 1 clove garlic (minced)
- 1/2 cup onions (chopped)
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1 6-oz. can tomato paste
- 2 cups Dr Pepper
- 1 cup water
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/8 teaspoon oregano
- 1 pint cooked beans (optional)

## Directions
1. Place ground meat in large heavy saucepan with onions and garlic. Cook, stirring with fork to break up meat, until slightly browned.
2. Add salt, tomato paste, Dr Pepper, and water. Cover, simmer until meat is very tender and mixture is desired thickness.
3. Add chili powder, paprika, oregano, pepper and beans (if desired). Cook 5 to 10 minutes longer. Serve very hot.

## Notes
- From a Dr Pepper recipe card (1966).
- Yield: 6 one-cup servings.
- Tip on the card: Garlic and other seasonings may be increased if stronger seasoning is desired.
