## Ingredients
- 2 cups raw fresh peanuts
- 1/2 cup white syrup (light corn syrup)
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons hot water
- 2 teaspoons soda
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Directions
1. Mix all and cook until peanuts pop.
2. Add soda and vanilla.
3. Cook until golden brown.
4. Pour on a buttered cookie sheet. Don't spread, just pour out — this is important!
