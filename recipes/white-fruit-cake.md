## Ingredients
- 2 cups of sugar
- 1 lb. of butter
- 8 eggs, beaten
- 4 cups of flour
- 1 tsp. of baking powder
- 1 lb. of chopped pecans
- 1 lb. chopped cherries
- 1 lb. of chopped pineapple
- 1 small can coconut
- 1 small lemon extract

## Directions
1. Bake in angel food cake pan, 250°, 3 hrs.
2. Or [half?] [an?] oblong pan, 1 1/2 hr @ 250°.
3. Put water in pan in bottom of oven.
