## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup cold milk
- 1/2 tsp. vanilla
- 1 envelope Dream Whip
- 1 pkg Lime Jell-O
- 2 Tbsp. sugar
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1 cup hot water
- Pineapple juice and water to make 1 cup
- 2/3 cup drained canned crushed pineapple
- 1 box small vanilla wafers

## Directions
1. Combine milk, vanilla, and Dream Whip; whip as directed on package. Chill.
2. Dissolve the Jell-O, sugar, and salt in hot water. Add juice and water and chill until slightly thickened.
3. Place in bowl of ice and water; beat until fluffy and thick.
4. Fold in Dream Whip and pineapple.
5. Pour into dessert dishes lined with wafers. Chill until firm.

## Notes
- Serves 8 to 10.
