## Ingredients
- 4 or 5 medium apples
- 1/4 cup Imperial Brown Sugar
- 2 Tbsp. butter
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 1 tsp. nutmeg
- 1 cup Imperial Pure Cane Granulated Sugar
- 1 cup sifted flour
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 Tbsp. melted butter

## Directions
1. Peel and slice apples in 8" x 8" x 2" square cake pan.
2. Sprinkle with Imperial Brown Sugar; dot with butter, then sprinkle with cinnamon and nutmeg.
3. Combine Imperial Pure Cane Granulated Sugar, flour, salt, baking powder, milk and melted butter into batter; pour over apple mixture.
4. Bake at 350° F. about 45 minutes.
5. Serve either hot or cold with whipped cream.

## Notes
- Makes 6 servings.
