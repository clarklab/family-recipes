## Ingredients
### Custard
- 4 egg yolks
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 3/4 c. sugar
- 2 cups milk
- 2 tbs. flour + a little water
- 1 tbs. butter

### Meringue
- 4 egg whites
- 1/3 c. sugar

## Directions
### Custard
1. No directions for the custard on the card.

### Meringue
1. Froth the 4 whites; add 1/3 c. sugar.
