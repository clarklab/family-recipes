## Ingredients
- 2 tsp. + 1/2 cups butter, softened & divided
- 1 can (5 oz.) evaporated [milk?]
- 2 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 cups (12 oz.) semisweet chips
- 1 jar marshmallow creme
- 24 caramels, quartered
- 3/4 cup salted cashew halves
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract

## Directions
1. Line 9 in. square baking pan [with?] foil; butter. Set aside.
2. [In?] a large heavy saucepan [combine?] milk, sugar & remaining [butter?].
3. [Cook?] & stir over medium heat until sugar is dissolved. Bring to a rapid boil; boil for 5 minutes, stirring constantly.
4. Remove from heat; stir in chocolate chips & marshmallow creme until melted. Fold in caramels, cashews & vanilla. Mix well.
5. Pour into prepared pan. Cool.
6. Remove from pan; cut into 1 in. squares. Store at room temperature.

## Notes
- Written on the back of an insurance payment receipt from 2001.
- The butter is divided: 2 tsp. for buttering the foil-lined pan, the rest goes into the saucepan.
