Applesauce Cake

Ingredients

  • 1 cup shortening
  • 2 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup applesauce
  • 2 cup flour
  • 1/4 tsp. salt
  • 1 tsp. B. Powder
  • 1/2 tsp. soda
  • 1 tsp. cinnamon
  • 1/2 cloves
  • 1/2 raisin and nut

Frosting

  • 1 lb. butter
  • 2 Tbsp. brown sugar
  • Cream
  • Powdered sugar

Directions

  1. Beat shortening, sugar, eggs, and applesauce well.
  2. Mix flour, salt, baking powder, soda, cinnamon, and cloves together, and add.
  3. Add raisins and nuts.
  4. Bake 350° 45 minutes.

Frosting

  1. No directions are given.
