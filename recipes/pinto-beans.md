## Ingredients
- 1 cup beans
- 3 cups water (for soaking)
- 3-4 cups water (for cooking)

## Directions
### Quick Soak
1. Bring 1 cup beans and 3 cups water to boil for 2 minutes.
2. Remove from heat. Cover & let stand 1 hour.
3. Drain soak water & rinse beans.

### Overnight Soak
1. Same amount beans & water (cold). Let it stand overnight or 8 hours.
2. Drain & rinse beans.

### Cooking
1. Add 3-4 cups water to rinsed and rinsed, simmer gently with lid tilted until desired tenderness is reached, 1 1/2 - 2 hours.
