## Ingredients
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 egg
- 1/3 cup shortening (heaping)
- 2 cups flour
- 1/2 tsp. soda
- 2 Tbsp. milk
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1/2 cup cherries (quartered)
- 1/2 cup chopped nuts

## Directions
1. Mix together brown sugar, egg, shortening, milk and vanilla.
2. Sift together flour and soda, and add.
3. Mix well and add cherries and nuts.
4. Bake on a greased cookie sheet at 375° for 10–12 minutes.

## Notes
- Amounts for a triple batch are written on the card: 3 cups brown sugar, 3 eggs, 1 cup shortening, 6 cups flour, 1 1/2 tsp. soda, 6 Tbsp. milk, 3 tsp. vanilla, 1 1/2 cups cherries, 1 1/2 cups nuts.
