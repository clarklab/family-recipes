Fruit Pemmican

Ingredients

  • 1 c. dried apricots
  • 1 c. raisins
  • 1 c. coconut, shredded fine
  • 1/2 c. dried prunes
  • 1 c. dried apples
  • 1 c. chopped nuts (walnuts)
  • 1/2 c. dates
  • 1/2 c. honey
  • 1/2 c. peanut butter
  • Water, if needed

Directions

  1. Put all ingredients through a food grinder at least twice.
  2. Blend in honey & peanut butter (if needed, 1 T. water at a time) until it has the consistency of very hard dough.
  3. Roll out in a thin roll; twist and cut in bite-sized pieces.

Notes
