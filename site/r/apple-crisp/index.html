Apple Crisp

Ingredients

  • 4 cups sliced cooking apples
  • 1 Tbsp. lemon juice
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • 1 cup oats
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp. cinnamon
  • 1/3 cup melted butter

Directions

  1. Place apples in greased shallow baking dish. Sprinkle with lemon juice.
  2. Combine dry ingredients. Add melted butter, mixing until crumbly.
  3. Sprinkle crumb mixture over apples.
  4. Bake in 375° F. 30 minutes or until apples are tender.

Notes

  • 6 servings.
