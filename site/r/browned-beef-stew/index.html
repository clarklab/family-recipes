Browned Beef Stew

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds boneless stew meat (beef)
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 3 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 3 tablespoons shortening or beef suet
  • 2 cups beef stock or bouillon
  • 2 cups Dr Pepper
  • 2 cups carrots, chunks
  • 1 1/2 cups onions, chunks
  • 3 cups potatoes, chunks
  • 1 cup (1/2-inch) celery crescents
  • 1 cup frozen or fresh garden peas

Directions

  1. Sprinkle meat with the salt and pepper and dust with the 1/4 cup flour.
  2. In a large stew kettle, brown meat in the 3 tablespoons fat until very brown.
  3. Add beef stock or bouillon and Dr Pepper and cook at a low temperature until meat is tender.
  4. Add chunked vegetables: carrots, potatoes, onions and celery. Cook until vegetables are beginning to be tender.
  5. Add frozen peas and cook at least 10 minutes longer.

Notes

  • From a Dr Pepper recipe card (1966).
  • Yield: 8 servings.
  • If stew must be reheated, heat in a slow oven until hot.
