Pizza Dough

From the kitchen of Betty Workman / Viki Love

Ingredients

  • 1 pkg. dry yeast (1 TB)
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 tsp. sugar
  • 1 tsp. salt
  • 2 TB salad oil
  • 2 1/2 c. flour

Directions

  1. Dissolve yeast in warm water. Stir in remaining dough ingredients.
  2. Beat vigorously about 20 strokes.
  3. Allow dough to rest about 5-10 min.
  4. Top with spaghetti sauce & mozzarella cheese.
  5. Bake 450° - 12 min.

Notes

  • From the kitchen of Betty Workman / Viki Love, '87.
  • Makes two 12-inch pizza crusts.
  • Written on a "Here's what's cooking" card with a cornucopia and the verse Joel 2:26.
