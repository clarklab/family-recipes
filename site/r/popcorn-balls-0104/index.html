Popcorn Balls

Ingredients

  • 1/2 c. wh. syrup
  • 1/2 c. wh. sugar
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • Popcorn (about 3 1/2 small batches)

Directions

  1. Mix ingred. in kettle; boil for 1 min.
  2. Pour over popcorn. Form into balls.

Notes

  • "Wh." means white: white syrup is light corn syrup, and white sugar is granulated sugar.
