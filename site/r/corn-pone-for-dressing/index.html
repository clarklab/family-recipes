Corn Pone for Dressing

Ingredients

Corn Pone

  • 3 cups corn meal
  • 3 cups flour
  • 2 tsp. salt
  • 8 tsp. baking powder
  • 4 1/2 cups milk
  • 1/4 cup cooking oil
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 Tbs. sage

Dressing

  • Turkey broth or chicken broth
  • Onions and celery
  • Butter
  • 4 chopped boiled eggs

Gravy

  • Broth from turkey
  • Water
  • 4 heaping tablespoons flour
  • 1 cup milk (or cream)
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Directions

Corn Pone

  1. Bake @ 425° 1 hour. Can bake ahead of time.

Dressing

  1. Add turkey broth or chicken broth.
  2. Sauté onions & celery in butter.
  3. Add 4 chopped boiled eggs to corn pone already cooked.
  4. Heat before serving.

Gravy

  1. Make paste with water & flour (4 heaping tablespoons flour, mixed to a paste).
  2. Add to hot broth & boil.
  3. Add milk or cream (1 cup milk). Salt & pepper to taste.

Notes

  • Also headed "Corn pone for dressing turkey or chicken."
  • Amounts for a larger batch (marked "20") are written in a column beside the ingredients: 4, 4, 3, 12, (scribbled out), 6, 3/4, 6, 1 1/2.
