## Ingredients
### Dip
- 2 1/2 T parsley - crushed
- 1 T onion salt
- 2 T salt
- 1/2 t garlic salt
- 1/2 t black pepper
- 1/2 T Accent
- 1 pt mayonnaise

### Salad Dressing
- 1 pt. buttermilk

## Directions
### Dip
1. Mix dry ingredients.
2. Mix 1 pt mayonnaise & dry ingredients.

### Salad Dressing
1. To make salad dressing, add 1 pt. buttermilk.
2. Leave uncovered 24 hrs in refrigerator. Put in 2 pt jars.

## Notes
- "T" is tablespoon and "t" is teaspoon.
- Accent is a seasoning (MSG).
