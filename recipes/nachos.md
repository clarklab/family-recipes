## Ingredients
- Corn tortillas
- Oil (about 1 cup)
- Salt
- Velveeta cheese
- 1/3 cup milk

## Directions
1. Cut corn tortillas up in triangles.
2. Put oil in pan - about 1 cup. Heat. Brown. Salt to taste.
3. Melt Velveeta cheese w/ 1/3 cup milk over low heat. Pour or dip w/ chips. (nacho)
