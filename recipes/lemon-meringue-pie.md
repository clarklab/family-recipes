## Ingredients
- 7 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 2 cups boiling water
- 3 eggs, separated
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 2 Tbsp. butter or margarine
- 1 Tbsp. grated lemon peel
- Baked and cooled 9-inch pastry shell

## Directions
1. Combine cornstarch, sugar, and salt. Add the water, and cook until thick, stirring constantly.
2. Cover and place over boiling water for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Beat egg yolks slightly, add a little of the hot mixture, stir, and pour back. Cook two minutes, stirring constantly.
4. Remove from heat and stir in lemon juice, butter or margarine, and grated lemon peel. Pour into pie shell.
