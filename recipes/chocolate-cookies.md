## Ingredients
- 1 cube butter
- 3 Tbsp. sugar
- 1 can Borden's Eagle Brand milk
- 1 - 12 oz. pkg. chocolate chips
- 1 c. flour
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 c. ground pecans or walnuts

## Directions
1. Put butter, sugar, chocolate chips and Eagle Brand milk in top of double boiler. Stir until melted.
2. Remove from fire and add the flour, vanilla & nuts.
3. Mix well & drop by teaspoonful on greased cookie sheet.
4. Bake at 350° for 8-10 min.
