## Ingredients
- 2 small onions, thinly sliced
- 3 pkg. (10 oz. each) frozen baby lima beans, thawed
- 2 cans (10 3/4 oz. each) condensed cream of celery soup
- 2 cans (4 oz. each) sliced mushrooms, undrained
- 1 jar (2 oz.) chopped pimiento, drained
- 1/8 t. pepper
- 1/2 t. dill seed
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese

## Directions
1. Combine all ingredients except heavy cream and Parmesan cheese in Crock-Pot; stir well.
2. Cover and cook on low setting for 10 to 12 hours. Or low 5 hours.
3. Just before serving, add cream and stir well; sprinkle Parmesan cheese on top.

## Notes
- Makes 8 to 10 servings (about 3 qts.).
