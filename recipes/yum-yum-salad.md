## Ingredients
- 2 c. crushed pineapple
- 1/2 c. sugar
- 1 small box Jello
- 1/2 c. boiling water
- 1 c. grated cheese (Cheddar)
- 1/2 c. chopped pecans
- 1 ctn. whipping cream, beaten stiff

## Directions
1. Mix pineapple and sugar. Heat until sugar dissolves. Remove from heat.
2. Mix Jello and boiling water. Mix this with pineapple mixture and let set until jelled (not hard).
3. Add cheese, pecans and whipped cream. Mix all together and put in mold. Let set 12-24 hours.
