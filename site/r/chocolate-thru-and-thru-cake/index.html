Chocolate Thru & Thru Cake

Ingredients

Cake

  • 1/4 cup cocoa
  • 1 cup boiling water
  • 2 tsp. soda
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 2/3 cup shortening
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 2 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp. vanilla

Frosting

  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 tsp. cream of tartar
  • 2 Tblsp. cocoa
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 lb. (2 sticks) butter
  • 1 tsp. vanilla

Directions

Cake

  1. Mix cocoa, boiling water and soda and let stand for 30 minutes (mix by hand).
  2. Mix (electric mixer) sugar, shortening, eggs, buttermilk, flour and vanilla.
  3. Bake @ 400° for 20 min.

Frosting

  1. Either cook in a double boiler for 30 minutes or slowly over burner, stirring constantly.
  2. After cooking 30 minutes, mix with electric mixer until stiff.
