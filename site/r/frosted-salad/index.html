Frosted Salad

Ingredients

  • 2 pkgs. lemon flavored gelatin
  • 2 cups boiling water
  • 2 cups clear carbonated beverage
  • 1 can (20 oz.) crushed pineapple
  • 1 cup miniature marshmallows
  • 2 large bananas, sliced
  • Whipped cream topping (below)

Topping

  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 Tbsp. flour
  • 1 cup pineapple juice
  • 1 egg, slightly beaten
  • 2 Tbsp. butter
  • 1 cup whipping cream, whipped
  • 1/4 cup shredded American cheese
  • 3 Tbsp. grated Parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. Dissolve gelatin in boiling water. Stir in carbonated beverage. Chill until partly set. Drain pineapple, saving the juice for the topping.
  2. Fold pineapple, marshmallows and bananas into gelatin. Pour into a 9-inch springform pan or 7 x 12" oblong dish. Chill until firm. Add topping. Chill overnight.

Topping

  1. Combine sugar and flour. Stir in pineapple juice and egg. Cook over low heat until thickened.
  2. Remove from heat; add butter. Let cool; chill.
  3. Fold into whipped cream. Frost gelatin mixture. Sprinkle with cheese. Chill overnight.
