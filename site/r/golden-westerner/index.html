Golden Westerner

Ingredients

  • 3 sticks butter or margarine
  • 1 1-lb. carton Imperial 10X Powdered Sugar
  • 6 eggs
  • 1 Imperial 10X Powdered Sugar carton full of sifted flour
  • 1 tsp. lemon juice
  • 1 Tbsp. vanilla

Directions

  1. Have eggs and butter at room temperature.
  2. Cream butter; add Imperial 10X Powdered sugar; beat till fluffy.
  3. Add eggs, one at a time; beat well after each addition.
  4. Sift flour 3 times; add gradually to batter. Add flavorings and mix well.
  5. Bake in 10" greased and floured tube pan at 325° 1 1/2 hours.
  6. Cool 4 minutes, then invert pan.
  7. Wonderful served plain, with whipped cream, fruit or ice cream. If you wish, spoon Imperial 10X Powdered Sugar lightly into ridges on top of cake.

Notes

  • Subtitled "Big Cake ... little time".
  • The flour is measured by refilling the empty powdered-sugar carton.
