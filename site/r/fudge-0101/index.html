Fudge

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. processed American cheese (cubed)
  • 1 lb. oleo
  • 1 tbs. vanilla
  • 1 cup cocoa
  • 4 lb. powdered sugar
  • 3 cup chopped pecans

Directions

  1. In heavy saucepan, melt cheese + oleo over low flame.
  2. Stir in vanilla. Sift cocoa + powdered sugar; stir into cheese mixture until smooth.
  3. Add pecans. Pour in buttered dish. Chill.

Notes

  • Makes 7 lbs.
  • Oleo is margarine.
  • The back of the card says "SALADS", as if it once marked a section of the recipe box.
