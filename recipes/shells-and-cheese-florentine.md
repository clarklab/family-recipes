## Ingredients
- 1 package (12 ounces) Velveeta Shells & Cheese
- 3 cups shredded spinach
- 1 cup mushroom slices
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 2 tablespoons Parkay Margarine
- Fresh spinach

## Directions
1. Prepare Shells and Cheese as directed on package except adding spinach to blanch during last 30 seconds of boiling Shell Macaroni. Continue as directed on package.
2. Saute mushrooms and onion in margarine. Add to Shells and Cheese and spinach, mix well.
3. Serve over bed of spinach leaves.

## Notes
- From a Kraft Velveeta recipe leaflet.
- Makes 6 servings. Prep time: 10 minutes. Cooking time: 15 minutes.
