Vicki Maize Cornbread

From the kitchen of Vicki Maize

Ingredients

  • Martha White mix (cornbread mix)
  • 1 egg
  • Milk

Filling

  • 1 lb. hamburger meat (brown)
  • 1 can cream corn
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 1 can green chili, mild
  • 1 pk. Mexican shredded cheese

Directions

  1. Brown the hamburger meat. Mix together with the cream corn, onion, green chili and cheese.
  2. Mix cornbread mix + 1 egg + milk.
  3. Pour 1/2 cornbread mix into greased baking pan, then add other mix in the center. Cover with the balance of cornbread mix.
  4. Cook 40 - 60 minutes @ 350°.

Notes
