Skor

Ingredients

  • 1 c butter
  • 1 c brown sugar
  • 1 egg white
  • 1 c flour
  • 6 Hershey's chocolate bars

Directions

  1. Cream butter, brown sugar and egg white. Add flour.
  2. Put into a greased big flat baking pan (15 x 10). Cook 15-20 minutes at 350°.
  3. When it comes out hot, lay 6 Hershey's chocolate bars on top and spread them over it as they soften.
  4. Let cool, then break into pieces.

Notes
