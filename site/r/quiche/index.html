Quiche

Ingredients

  • 9" pie crust (frozen)
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 3/4 cup cheese, or Gruyère (2 ounces)
  • 4–5 slices cooked bacon, crisp
  • Salt / pepper
  • 1/4 tsp. thyme

Directions

  1. Cook approx. 30 min. @ 350° till firm.
