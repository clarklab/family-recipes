Chocolate Ricotta Cheesecake

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup graham cracker crumbs
  • 3 1/2 cups part-skim ricotta cheese, drained
  • Yogurt cheese* (made from 16-oz. container vanilla lowfat yogurt, no gelatin added)
  • 2 egg whites
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup Hershey's Cocoa
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 325°. Sprinkle crumbs on bottom of 9-inch springform pan.
  2. In food processor bowl or large mixer bowl, process ricotta cheese until smooth.
  3. Add yogurt cheese, egg whites, sugar, cocoa, flour and vanilla; process just until well blended. Pour over crumbs.
  4. Bake 50 minutes or until edges are set. Turn off oven; open door slightly. Leave cheesecake in oven 1 hour.
  5. Remove from oven. Cool; refrigerate until thoroughly chilled.

Yogurt Cheese

  1. *To make yogurt cheese: Line a non rusting colander or sieve with a large piece of double thickness cheesecloth; place colander over a deep bowl.
  2. Spoon yogurt into prepared colander; cover. Refrigerate until liquid no longer drains from yogurt, about 24 hours.
  3. Remove yogurt from cheesecloth; discard liquid.

Notes

  • From a Hershey's Cocoa label leaflet, "The Lean Way to Make it Chocolate! 8 Light & Luscious Recipes."
  • 16 servings. Per serving: 160 calories, 20 mg cholesterol, 5 g fat.
