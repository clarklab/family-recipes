Mitchel Corn Bread

Ingredients

  • 1 can creamed corn
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 1 pk. shredded cheese
  • Jalapeño or chili for flavor
  • Meat: browned hamburger, or chopped & cooked chicken, or chopped & cooked turkey

Directions

  1. Cook 40-50 minutes @ 350°.

Notes

  • Written beside the title: "no eggs for freezing."
  • A variation of the Vicki Maize Cornbread recipe on the other side of this card.
