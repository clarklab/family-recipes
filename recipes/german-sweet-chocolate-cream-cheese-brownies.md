## Ingredients
### Brownie Layer
- 1 package (4 oz.) Baker's German's Sweet Chocolate
- 1/4 cup (1/2 stick) margarine
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup chopped nuts (optional)

### Cream Cheese Layer
- 4 ounces Philadelphia Brand Cream Cheese, softened
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour

## Directions
### Brownie Layer
1. Heat oven to 350° F.
2. Microwave* chocolate and margarine in large microwavable bowl at HIGH 2 minutes or until margarine is melted. Stir until chocolate is completely melted.
3. Stir sugar into melted chocolate mixture. Stir in eggs and vanilla until completely mixed. Mix in flour until well blended. Stir in nuts. Spread in greased 8-inch square pan.

### Cream Cheese Layer
1. In same bowl, mix cream cheese, sugar, egg and flour until smooth. Spoon over brownie mixture; swirl with knife to marbleize.
2. Bake for 35 minutes or until wooden pick inserted in center comes out with fudgy crumbs. (Do not overbake.) Cool in pan; cut into squares.

## Notes
- From a Baker's "One Bowl Recipe" card.
- *Saucepan preparation: Melt chocolate and margarine in 2-quart saucepan over very low heat; stir constantly just until melted. Remove from heat. Continue as above.
- Makes 16 brownies.
