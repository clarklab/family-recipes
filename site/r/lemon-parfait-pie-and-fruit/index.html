Lemon Parfait Pie and Fruit

Ingredients

  • 1 pkg. jello - lemon, straw. or rasp.
  • 1 1/4 cup hot water
  • 3 Tbsp. lemon juice
  • Rind one lemon
  • 1 pt. vanilla ice cream

Directions

  1. Dissolve jello in hot water.
  2. Add lemon juice, rind and ice cream.
  3. Let thicken then pour into shell.
