Penuche

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 c. sugar
  • 1 c. brown sugar
  • 1/3 c. light cream
  • 1/3 c. milk
  • 2 Tbsp. butter
  • 1 tsp. vanilla
  • 1/2 c. broken nuts

Directions

  1. Butter the sides of a heavy 2-qt. saucepan. In it combine sugars, cream, milk and butter.
  2. Heat over medium heat, stirring constantly, till sugars dissolve and mixture comes to boiling.
  3. Cook to soft ball stage (238°), stirring only if necessary.
  4. Immediately remove from heat and cool to lukewarm (110°). Do not stir.
  5. Add vanilla. Beat vigorously till candy becomes very thick and starts to lose its gloss.
  6. Quickly stir in nuts and spread in a buttered shallow pan. Score while warm; cut when firm.

Notes

  • Dated 2008. Ends with "Enjoy" and a little heart.
