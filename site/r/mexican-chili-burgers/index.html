Mexican Chili Burgers

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 medium (1/2 cup) onion, chopped
  • 1 can (4 ounces) chopped green chilies, drained
  • 1 tablespoon red pepper sauce
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1/4 cup dry bread crumbs
  • 6 hamburger buns
  • Lettuce leaves
  • 6 Velveeta Slices Process Cheese Spread
  • Salsa

Directions

  1. Combine first six ingredients. Form into 6 patties.
  2. Broil 5 minutes on each side or until desired doneness.
  3. Top with process cheese spread; continue broiling until melted.
  4. For each sandwich, layer lettuce, cooked cheeseburger and salsa on hamburger bun.

Notes

  • From a Kraft Velveeta recipe leaflet.
  • Makes 6 servings. Prep time: 15 minutes. Cooking time: 12 minutes.
