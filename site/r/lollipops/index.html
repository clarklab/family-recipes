Lollipops

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of sugar
  • 1 cup of Karo
  • 2 cups of water
  • Flavoring
  • Coloring

Directions

  1. Boiled together until a drop becomes brittle when dropped in water.
  2. Pour drops on table and stick lollipop sticks in them.
