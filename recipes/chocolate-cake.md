## Ingredients
- [1/2?] cup cocoa
- 2 cups boiling water
- 4 tsp soda
- 4 cups sugar
- [1 1/2?] cup butter
- 6 eggs
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 5 cups flour
- [2?] tsp vanilla

### Frosting
- 4 cups sugar
- 2 tsp cream of tartar
- 4 tbsp cocoa
- 4 eggs
- 4 sticks butter
- 2 tsp vanilla

## Directions
1. Mix cocoa, boiling water and soda & let stand for 30 min.
2. Mix sugar, butter and eggs.
3. Add buttermilk, flour and vanilla.
4. Bake 400 for 20 min.

### Frosting
1. Stir while bringing to boil (soft).
2. Mix with mixer until stiff.

## Notes
- These amounts make a double batch: the card is headed "2 Recipes of Chocolate Cake" and the frosting "Frosting — Double recipe".
- Other versions are on cards 0063 and 0129.
