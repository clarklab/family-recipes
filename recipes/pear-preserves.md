## Ingredients
- Pears
- Sugar
- Water

## Directions
1. Peel and cut pears up.
2. Soak them overnight in heavy sugar water (meaning quite a bit of sugar, like jelly).
3. Then cook them on low heat until thickened syrup consistency.
