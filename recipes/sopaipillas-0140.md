## Ingredients
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 level tsp salt
- 1 tbs lard or shortening
- Warm water (app. 12 tbs)

## Directions
1. Mix flour, baking powder, salt, sugar until well blended.
2. Add enough warm water to make a medium soft dough, like pie crust.
3. Place dough in covered bowl and allow to stand at room temperature for thirty minutes.
4. Then roll dough out on floured board evenly at 1/8 inch thickness. Cut into 3 inch squares and fry in deep hot fat until fluffy brown.
5. Remove from fat and drain on paper towel. Serve hot.

## Notes
- Makes about two dozen. Serve with honey, or sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon.
