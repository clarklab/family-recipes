## Ingredients
- 1 gal. cucumbers
- 8 small onions, white
- 2 green peppers, shredded
- 1/2 cup salt
- Ice

### Syrup
- 5 cups sugar
- 1/2 tsp. gr. cloves
- 1 tsp. celery seed
- 1 1/2 tsp. turmeric
- 2 Tbsp. mustard seed
- 5 cups vinegar

## Directions
1. Select crisp cucumbers. Wash (but do not pare). Slice criss cross in paper thin slices. Slice onions and peppers.
2. Mix salt with vegetables and bury pieces of ice among veg. Cover with a weight and let stand 3 hours.

### Syrup
1. Mix sugar and spices and vinegar and pour over cucumbers.
2. Place over low heat, stir occasionally. Heat to scalding but do not boil.
3. Seal in jars.
