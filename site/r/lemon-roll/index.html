Lemon Roll

Ingredients

Filling

  • 6 Tbsp. lemon juice
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1 1/2 sticks butter or margarine
  • Grated rind of 2 lemons
  • 3 eggs, well beaten

Roll

  • 4 eggs, at room temperature
  • 1/8 tsp. salt
  • 1 tsp. baking powder
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup sifted cake flour
  • 1/2 tsp. lemon extract
  • Powdered sugar, for dusting

Directions

Filling

  1. Combine lemon juice, sugar, butter and lemon rind in top of double boiler and stir over hot water until blended.
  2. Add eggs and stir continuously until thick, about 15 minutes.
  3. Refrigerate at least 4 hrs.

Roll

  1. Grease, line with wax paper and grease again a 10" by 15" baking sheet.
  2. Beat eggs, salt and baking powder until fluffy. Add sugar very slowly, beating continuously, until very thick.
  3. Fold in lemon extract, then flour. Pour in prepared pan.
  4. Bake at 375° for 13–15 min.
  5. Invert roll on wax paper heavily dusted with powdered sugar. Pull away baked paper. Cool slightly.
  6. Spread with lemon filling and form into a roll. For a tight, neat roll use only part of the filling and save the rest for little tarts or to spread on pound cake. For a rich, cozy one, use it all.

Notes

  • From the card: the filling "turns a simple jelly roll into a rich and elegant dessert," and it keeps well "(provided you hide it)," so it can be made the day before.
  • With the full filling, it serves 8 or 10 easily.
