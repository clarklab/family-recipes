## Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 1 cup Karo syrup
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup pecan halves
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 unbaked pie shell
- Salt

## Directions
1. Beat eggs & sugar until thick.
2. Add vanilla, Karo & salt. Mix; pour over nuts in shell.
3. Bake at 300° for 50 to 60 min.
