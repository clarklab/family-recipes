Potato Latkes

Ingredients

  • 2 large white potatoes
  • 1/2 onion
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • Oil for frying

Directions

  1. Wash, scrub and cut potatoes into proper size for food processor or blender. Do not peel!
  2. Add all ingredients to processor or blender, then use on/off control until blended but not chopped too fine.
  3. Pour mixture into bowl.
  4. Heat oil in skillet.
  5. Drop mixture by tablespoons into hot skillet and fry on both sides until brown.
  6. Serve plain or with applesauce or sour cream.

Notes

  • From a newspaper Hanukkah feature: "Here's the recipe for the Brachman's latkes."
  • Nutrition per latke: 156 calories, 11 grams fat, 27 milligrams cholesterol, 159 milligrams sodium, 64 percent of calories from fat.
