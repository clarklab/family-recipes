Silky Cocoa Creme

Ingredients

  • 1 envelope unflavored gelatin
  • 1/4 cup cold water
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup Hershey's Cocoa
  • 1/2 cup part-skim ricotta cheese
  • 3/4 cup skim milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup frozen light non-dairy whipped topping, thawed
  • Fresh strawberries

Directions

  1. In small bowl, sprinkle gelatin over water; allow to stand 2 minutes to soften.
  2. In medium saucepan, stir together sugar and cocoa; stir in milk. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until mixture is very hot.
  3. Add gelatin mixture, stirring until gelatin is dissolved; pour into medium bowl. Refrigerate until mixture is chilled (do not allow to gel).
  4. In blender container or food processor bowl, beat ricotta cheese and vanilla until smooth; stir into whipped topping.
  5. Gradually fold into cocoa mixture; pour into 2-cup mold. Refrigerate until set.
  6. Unmold; serve with strawberries.

Notes

  • From a Hershey's Cocoa label leaflet, "The Lean Way to Make it Chocolate!"
  • Makes 8 servings. Per serving: 110 calories, 5 mg cholesterol, 3 g fat.
