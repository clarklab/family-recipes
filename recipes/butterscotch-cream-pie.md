## Ingredients
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1 cup water
- 1 2/3 cup milk
- 1 cup (packed) brown sugar
- 1/2 cup cake flour
- 1/3 cup butter
- 3 egg yolks, slightly beaten
- 1 1/2 tsp. vanilla

## Directions
1. Mix sugar, flour and salt in sauce pan. Gradually stir in water and milk. Add butter.
2. Cook over med. heat, stirring constantly, until mixture thickens and boils. Boil 1 minute. Remove from heat.
3. Gradually stir 1/2 of hot mixture into egg yolks. Blend into hot mixture in sauce pan.
4. Boil 1 minute more, stirring constantly.
5. Add vanilla and pour into pie shell.
