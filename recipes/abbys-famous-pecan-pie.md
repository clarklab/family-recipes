## Ingredients
- 1 (9-inch) unbaked pie crust
- 1 cup light corn syrup
- 1 cup firmly packed dark brown sugar
- 3 eggs, slightly beaten
- 1/3 cup butter, melted
- 1/3 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 heaping cup pecan halves

## Directions
1. Heat oven to 350°. Prepare pie crust.
2. In large bowl, combine corn syrup, sugar, eggs, butter, salt and vanilla; mix well.
3. Pour filling into prepared pie crust; sprinkle with pecan halves.
4. Bake at 350° for 45 to 50 minutes or until center is set. (Toothpick inserted will come out clean when pie is done.) Cool.
5. If crust or pie appears to be getting too brown, cover with foil for remaining baking time.

## Notes
- From a newspaper clipping. Serves 8 to 10.
- Amounts for a triple batch are written in the margin: 3 cups corn syrup, 3 cups brown sugar, 9 eggs, 1 cup butter, 1 tsp. salt, 3 tsp. vanilla, 3 cups pecans.
- Tip from the clipping: the bake time is for a gas oven; in an electric oven it may take 15 to 20 minutes longer. Start testing with a toothpick after 45 minutes.
- Top with a bit of whipped cream or ice cream, but even plain, nothing tops this!
