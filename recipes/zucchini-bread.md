## Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 1 cup Crisco oil
- 2 C. sugar
- 2 C. zucchini squash, grated
- 2 1/2 flour
- 1 tsp soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 teas. baking powder
- 1 heaping tablespoon cinnamon
- 1 cup nuts
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Directions
1. 350° 1 hr.

## Notes
- Makes two loaves or 1 Bundt cake.
