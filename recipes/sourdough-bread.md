## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. dry yeast
- 1/2 c. warm water
- 1/3 c. sugar
- 1 c. starter
- 1 egg or 1/3 c. oil
- 1 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1 c. warm water
- 5 or 6 c. flour

### Starter feeding
- 1 c. milk
- 1 c. flour
- 1/3 c. sugar

## Directions
1. Dissolve yeast in 1/2 c. warm water and 1/3 c. sugar. Let sit 15 min.
2. Add 1 c. starter. Let set 15 min.
3. Add 1 egg or 1/3 c. oil; 1 1/2 tsp. salt; 1 c. warm water; 5 or 6 c. flour. Knead until elastic.
4. Let rise 2 hrs. Punch down and shape into loaves.
5. Let rise 30 min. Bake at 350° 30 to 40 min. Makes 2 loaves.

### Starter feeding
1. Use once a week or feed — wait 24 hrs.
