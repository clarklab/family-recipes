Prickly Pear Jelly

Ingredients

  • 4 1/2 cups juice
  • 1/2 cup lemon juice
  • 1 box Sure-Jell
  • 6 1/2 cups of sugar

Directions

  1. Combine juice, Sure-Jell, lemon juice. Bring to a fast boil.
  2. Add sugar when it comes to a fast boil that cannot be stirred down.
  3. Then boil 2 minutes. Transfer, skim & pour up.
  4. Seal with lids or paraffin.

Notes
