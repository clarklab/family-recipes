## Ingredients
- 1 cup light corn syrup
- 1 cup light brown sugar
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 1/2 cup milk
- 3 Tbsp. butter
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Directions
1. Combine ingredients in saucepan.
2. Cook over medium heat to a full rolling boil and continue cooking for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Cool and spoon on servings of cake or ice cream-topped cake.
