## Ingredients
- 1/3 cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 1/2 cups chopped raisins
- 3 1/2 Tbsp. flour
- 3 egg yolks
- 2 cups water
- 1/2 tsp. lemon extract
- 3 egg whites
- 2 Tbsp. powdered sugar
- 1/4 tsp. orange extract

## Directions
1. Cook in double boiler until thick. Pour in baked shells.
2. 325° for 12 minutes.
