## Ingredients
- 1 small chicken

### Dumplings
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 heaping tablespoon shortening
- 1/2 cup milk

## Directions
1. Cook chicken for 45 min @ 10# pressure.

### Dumplings
1. Mix flour, salt, baking powder and shortening. Add milk. Knead into thick dough.
2. Roll out into squares (1"-1 1/2").
3. In a separate bowl, clean chicken from bones, skin etc.
4. Bring chicken broth to rolling boil. Drop squares into rolling boil one at a time.
5. Cook until squares are not [doughy?].

## Notes
- "10#" means 10 pounds of pressure in the pressure cooker.
