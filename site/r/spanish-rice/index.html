Spanish Rice

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cup tomatoes
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup chopped onions
  • 1/3 cup chopped green peppers
  • 1/2 cup chopped celery
  • 3 tbs oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 bay leaf

Directions

  1. Sauté onions, green pepper and celery in oil.
  2. Add other ingredients and simmer until thick.

Notes

  • Serves 6.
