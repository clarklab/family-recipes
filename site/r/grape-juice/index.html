Grape Juice

Ingredients

  • Grapes
  • Water
  • Sugar (enough for your taste)

Directions

  1. Put water with grapes and enough sugar (for your taste) and cook them.
  2. Run them through a colander, then cheesecloth.

Notes

  • From Mom: "For the grape juice - she just puts water with grapes and enough sugar (for your taste) & cooks them."
