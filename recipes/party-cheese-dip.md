## Ingredients
- 2 cups (16 oz.) cottage cheese
- 3 oz. cream cheese
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 1 Tbsp. grated onion
- 1 tsp. seasoning salt
- Few drops hot pepper sauce
- 1 tsp. soy sauce
- 1 can (3 2/3 oz) or (1/2 cup) smoked oysters, drained and chopped

## Directions
1. Beat together cottage cheese, cream cheese and sour cream until well blended.
2. Add onion, seasoning salt, hot pepper sauce, soy sauce and oysters; beat well.
3. Chill for several hours.

## Notes
- Makes about 3 1/4 cups.
