## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup tequila or chicken broth
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 lb. Velveeta Mexican Process Cheese Spread with Jalapeno Pepper, cubed
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro
- For dipping: cooked chicken chunks, jicama chunks, red, green or yellow pepper chunks, tortilla chips

## Directions
1. Mix together tequila and garlic in 1 1/2 quart bowl. Microwave on HIGH 2-3 minutes or until hot.
2. Stir in process cheese spread and lime juice.
3. Microwave 3 to 4 minutes or until process cheese spread is melted, stirring every 2 minutes.
4. Stir in cilantro.
5. Keep fondue hot while serving. Dip cooked chicken chunks, jicama chunks, red, green or yellow pepper chunks and tortilla chips into fondue.

### Conventional
1. Heat tequila and garlic in medium saucepan or fondue pot. Bring to boil. Reduce heat; simmer 1 minute.
2. Add process cheese spread and lime juice; stir until process cheese spread is melted.
3. Stir in cilantro. Serve as directed.

## Notes
- From a Kraft Velveeta recipe leaflet.
- Makes 4 servings. Prep time: 5 minutes. Microwave cooking time: 7 minutes. Conventional cooking time: 5 minutes.
