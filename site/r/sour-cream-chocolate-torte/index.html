Sour Cream Chocolate Torte

Ingredients

  • 1 cup boiling water
  • 3 squares (1 oz. each) unsweetened chocolate
  • 2 cups sifted flour
  • 2 tsp. baking soda
  • 1 tsp. salt
  • 1 cup dairy sour cream
  • 1 tsp. vanilla extract
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup firmly packed light brown sugar
  • 1 cup (1/2 pt.) heavy cream, whipped
  • 3 Tbsp. confectioners sugar
  • Confectioners sugar

Directions

  1. Pour water over chocolate in saucepan. Heat to boiling and cook gently until mixture starts to thicken, stirring occasionally. Remove from heat and cool.
  2. Sift together flour, baking soda and salt. Combine sour cream and vanilla.
  3. Beat together eggs, sugar and brown sugar until thick and creamy.
  4. Add flour mixture to egg mixture alternately with sour cream mixture, beginning and ending with flour. Blend in chocolate.
  5. Turn into 2 greased wax paper lined 8-inch round cake pans. Bake in preheated 350° gas oven, 30 to 35 minutes, or until done.
  6. Cool in pans 10 minutes before removing. Cool completely and split each layer horizontally in half.
  7. Beat cream and 3 Tbsp. confectioners sugar together until stiff. Spread between cake layers.
  8. Place paper doily over top of cake and sprinkle with confectioners sugar. Remove doily carefully to leave design. Serve immediately or refrigerate until ready to serve.
