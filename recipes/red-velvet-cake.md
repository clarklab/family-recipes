## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup Crisco
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 1 tsp. salt
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups flour
- 1 Tbsp. vinegar
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 2 oz. red cake coloring
- 1 Tbsp. cocoa
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 tsp. soda
- Icing (below)

### Icing
- 1 cup milk
- Pinch of salt
- 1 cup Crisco
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 tsp. vanilla

## Directions
1. Combine shortening and sugar, add coloring, eggs, vanilla.
2. Sift flour, cocoa and salt together 3 times. Add alternately with buttermilk.
3. Last, mix vinegar with soda and fold into batter. Do not beat.
4. Pour into greased and floured pans; bake in 350° oven 35 minutes.

### Icing
1. Mix flour, salt and milk and cook until thick, cool. This is very important.
2. Beat sugar and shortening at high speed until fluffy.
3. Add to cooled mixture, beat again until light and fluffy. Add vanilla.
4. May be garnished with coconut, nuts or fruit.
